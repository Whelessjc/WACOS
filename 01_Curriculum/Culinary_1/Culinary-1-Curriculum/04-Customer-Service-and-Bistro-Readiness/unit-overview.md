# Unit 04: Customer Service and Bistro Readiness

## Weeks Covered
Week 4 (August 31-September 4, 2026)

## Focus
Understand the full flow of service.

## Purpose
Students learn Life of an Order from guest arrival to farewell: greeting, order taking, ticket flow, cooking, expo, food running, check back, clearing, dish, reset, station assignments, station training, mock service, Bistro readiness, and stewardship of guest and work spaces. Friday, September 4 is an early-release day. The Week 4 endpoint is training and mock service only; there is no public service.

## Learning Targets
- I can explain how an order moves through service.
- I can describe basic guest-facing hospitality.
- I can follow my station assignment during practice service.
- I can communicate ticket status clearly.
- I can help reset the room after service.

## Core Skills
- Greeting and farewell
- Order taking basics
- Ticket flow
- Station roles
- Expo and food running
- Clearing and dish flow
- Service reset
- Stewardship of guest and kitchen spaces

## Vocabulary
| Term | Student-Friendly Meaning |
|---|---|
| Hospitality | Making people feel respected and cared for. |
| Life of an Order | The path an order takes from guest arrival to reset. |
| Ticket | The order information the team uses to prepare food. |
| Expo | The place or person organizing food before it goes out. |
| Food runner | Person who delivers food to the guest or service point. |
| Mock service | A practice service without public-service claims or pressure. |

## Lab Applications
- Station training
- Mock service
- Bistro prep
- Full mock service

## Bistro Connection
This is the first full service connection. Students see how kitchen skills, sanitation, communication, and hospitality work together before the first normal public service in Week 5.

## Assessment Ideas
- Life of an Order explanation
- Station role observation
- Mock service communication check
- Mock-service debrief
- Reset inspection

## Teacher Notes
Keep the early-release Friday small and controlled. The goal is flow and role clarity, not speed or menu complexity. The first normal public service target is Friday, September 11 in Week 5, subject to readiness and safety.

## Resources Needed
- Equipment: station supplies, order slips, service tools, dish area, towels, sanitizer
- Ingredients: teacher-selected simple Bistro prep items
- Materials: station role list, sample tickets, service flow map if useful
