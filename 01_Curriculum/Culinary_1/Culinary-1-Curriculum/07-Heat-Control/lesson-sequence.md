# Lesson Sequence: Heat Control

**Current recurring resource:** October 17, 2026. Folder numbering does not establish a calendar block.

## When to Use

Heat instruction begins with Week 1 pancakes and precedes each method/equipment use. Emphasize oven work Week 8, eggs/components Week 9, pans Week 10 and moist heat Week 11.

## Learning

- **Knowledge:** Heat/doneness cues, preheating, spacing/batches, boil versus simmer, moisture and timing.
- **Skill:** Observe, adjust and complete an approved application with equipment reset.
- **Professional Thinking:** Watch the food and operation; make purposeful adjustments.

## Practice Progression

1. Demonstrate the actual equipment, authorized use and handling/cleanup before student work. Pancakes provide early heat-cue practice; do not delay heat instruction to Week 10.

2. Repeat potatoes/oven work with cut size, tray spacing and hot-pan communication; repeat eggs with gentle heat and coordinated familiar components.

3. Use sauteed ingredients to observe pan/oil cues and crowding; compare effects within usable cooking rather than an unnecessary extra recipe.

4. Blanch/shock vegetables with the approved setup, tools, timing, draining and finishing; use other meetings for useful moist-heat work or correction rather than repeated blanching for its own sake.

5. Rice or another relevant simmered application may reinforce ratios and texture. Use verified recipes and storage controls. Grill/broiler use requires equipment-specific instruction, approved SOP and individual authorization; demonstration alone does not authorize students.

6. Route dishes while working, protect food, safely stop/shut down equipment and verify complete reset. Reduce batch size if maintenance or closing becomes unmanageable.
