# Lesson Sequence: Demonstrate Growth and Independence

**Current recurring resource:** October 17, 2026. Folder numbering does not establish a calendar block.

## When to Use

Weeks 17-18 consolidate evidence; planning, reflection and ownership begin in Week 1 and continue throughout.

## Learning

- **Knowledge:** Task requirements, demonstrated strengths, remaining gaps and useful next steps.
- **Skill:** Plan, initiate, monitor and complete a manageable familiar assignment through reset.
- **Professional Thinking:** Use evidence honestly; carry an improvement into the next performance.

## Practice Progression

1. Use actual individual evidence to select a relevant gap for Week 17 practice. Do not assume course attendance or group product success establishes competence.

2. Students write a useful workflow, use established references/supports and identify checkpoints; instructor verifies safe access and manageable scope.

3. Repeat the assignment with a specific improvement; capture direct performance in existing records rather than create another portfolio bureaucracy.

4. In Week 18, complete a manageable final practical from readiness and planning through protected product/reset. Existing assessment rules and accommodations govern; this resource adds no new grade weights.

5. Review demonstrated skills, necessary supports and next steps. Explain Culinary 2 as full-year depth/operations and fundamentals under pressure; participation does not automatically establish current competence.

6. Continue normal Prep/Service when calendar and readiness permit; use the actual final-week calendar rather than inventing an additional service requirement.
