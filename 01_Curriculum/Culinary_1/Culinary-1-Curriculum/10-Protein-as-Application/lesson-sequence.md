# Lesson Sequence: Protein as Application

**Current recurring resource:** October 17, 2026. Folder numbering does not establish a calendar block.

## When to Use

Week 2 established chicken-temperature application as appropriate; eggs before use in Week 3; coordinated eggs Week 9; chicken reinforcement Week 12. Safety precedes every earlier assignment.

## Learning

- **Knowledge:** Raw/clean separation, handling, heat/doneness evidence, thermometer technique and relevant portion expectations.
- **Skill:** Set up, cook under approved controls, verify, portion/protect and reset.
- **Professional Thinking:** Verify before food moves forward; prevent contamination of the next task.

## Practice Progression

1. Before any protein task, demonstrate relevant handling and raw/clean boundaries, equipment and thermometer cleaning/placement under current procedures.

2. Use scrambled eggs early where manageable. Teach cracking and any required separation as authentic micro-skills before use, plus heat control, tools and reset.

3. Repeat one relevant approved chicken method before adding another. Add a familiar side only when the students can manage the complete workload.

4. Use actual temperature/doneness evidence and adult checkpoints; never substitute appearance for the approved safety check. Follow existing time/temperature, cooling, holding and storage procedures.

5. Ground turkey, tofu or pan-sauce applications are available when useful and approved, not additional mandatory coverage. Do not recreate a late-semester protein block or displace Week 16 Chopped.

6. Include raw-contact sanitation, thermometer care, dish routing, food protection and station/shared reset in every practical check.
