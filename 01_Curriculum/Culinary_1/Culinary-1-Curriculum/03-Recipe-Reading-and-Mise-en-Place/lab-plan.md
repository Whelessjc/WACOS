# Lab Plan: Recipe Reading and Mise en Place

## Lab Purpose
Use simple recipes to practice reading, measuring, sequencing, prep lists, and organized station setup.

## Skill Focus
- Recipe annotation
- Ingredient and equipment lists
- Measuring
- Prep list creation
- Mise en place
- Order of operations
- Cookware sizing and care
- Substitution judgment
- Clean-as-you-go

## Ingredients or Materials
- Peanut butter bar ingredients
- Rice and liquid
- Fried rice or stir fry ingredients selected by teacher
- Teacher-approved dried pasta and basic tomato-sauce ingredients
- Food Handler review materials

## Equipment
- Measuring tools
- Mixing bowls
- Sheet pans
- Rice cooker or pots
- Saute pans or woks
- Large pot, colander, tongs, and saucepan
- Spatulas and spoons
- Towels and sanitizer

## Setup
- Display or distribute the recipe and the current Mystery Mise measuring-station passport.
- Arrange five station pairs across ten clearly numbered stations and post the five-team rotation order.
- Assign each team a starting pair. Students rotate only on the instructor signal.
- Stage recipe ingredients so they travel with the team; keep experiment/distractor materials at their assigned stations.
- Conduct the whole-group butter cutting and softening demonstration before rotations. Students do not cut butter at a station.
- Assign team roles and stage ingredients to reduce crowding.

## Procedure
1. Read the recipe completely and mark ingredients, equipment, steps, and timing.
2. Watch the instructor demonstrate cutting the required butter amount and softening it in a microwave-safe container using 10-second increments, stirring after each increment and repeating only until softened.
3. Begin at the assigned numbered station pair.
4. Read and follow each station's written directions before touching materials.
5. Complete the required measurement or thinking task and record the result in the passport.
6. Clean and reset the station.
7. Rotate to the next posted pair only on the instructor signal.
8. Continue until all ten stations and the student record are complete.
9. Build the prep list, organize the recipe tray, and complete the mise check.
10. Present the completed passport, recipe tray, and production plan at the instructor checkpoint.
11. Begin production only after instructor release.
12. For peanut butter pretzel bars, melt the chocolate chips and shortening in a microwave-safe container using 10-second increments, stirring after each increment and repeating only until melted and smooth.
13. Pause for additional instructor checks on sequence, dishes, towels, station control, and product handling as needed.
14. Clean cookware using the correct method, store the product as directed, and reset the station.

## Teacher Checkpoints
- Numbered stations, starting pairs, and rotation order are clear.
- Students remain at their assigned pair until the instructor signal.
- Completed passport and mise record are checked before production release.
- Recipe read before starting.
- Correct tools gathered.
- Cookware size matches the batch and method.
- Substitutions are approved rather than improvised.
- Prep list makes sense.
- Measuring is careful.
- Station stays organized.

## Cleanup and Reset Expectations
Tools returned, dishes routed correctly, counters sanitized, food stored or discarded as directed, towels handled, floor checked.

## Possible Variations
- Use rice only for ratio practice.
- Make fried rice teacher-led if heat control is not ready.
- Use dried pasta and basic tomato sauce to assess timing, doneness by feel, salting water, sequencing, and sauce mise en place. Confirm the formula and gluten substitution before scheduling.
