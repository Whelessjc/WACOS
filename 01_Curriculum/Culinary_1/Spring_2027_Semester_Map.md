## Specific daily replacements

- Week 9: Monday/Tuesday eggs with a familiar potato/vegetable component; Wednesday quarter-end deep clean with inspection/correction; Thursday Prep; Friday Service.
- Week 14: Monday Chopped appetizer, Tuesday entrée, Wednesday dessert; existing rules; Thursday/Friday operations when safe.
- Week 16: Tuesday event planning/rehearsal, Wednesday approved production/handoff, Thursday April 29 Teacher Appreciation meal, Friday complete close/review. Menu, count and handling capacity are not supplied by this map.
- Week 17: return to the first pancake formula with fewer prompts; target individual gaps and rehearse familiar final work. No automatic second Teacher Appreciation unit.
- Week 18: scoped familiar cleaning, production/handoff and meal/service evidence, corrections, reflection and initial breakdown. No assumed 200-guest final, Korean-beef menu or extra-class coverage.

## Year-end attendance and close

May 17–21 is the expected final student-attendance week: reflection, breakdown, cleaning, inventory, necessary demonstrations and planning for next year. May 24–27 is reserved for rare makeup and closeout/planning; no required new instruction depends on attendance. Retain May 27 as the published last student day and May 28 teacher workday; the instructor expectation does not amend the district calendar.
