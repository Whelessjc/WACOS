## Week 11 planned spine vs. readiness

The existing Culinary 1 heat-control lesson sequence proposes **Week 11: Roasting and Oven Control** (oven safety/spacing; roasted vegetables; browning, caramelization and texture; oven timing; Bistro application). This is a *planning option*, not a commitment to advance. The Week 10 recovery displaced the generic Week 10 pan-heat sequence; do not assume students completed it.

**Decision rule:** Keep or introduce roasting only if current food protection, dish flow, knife safety and complete closing are safe enough to support another hot-station task. If not, retain a familiar, low-complexity cooking vehicle and reteach the deficient procedure. Protect Bistro Prep/Service if safe and ready.

## Three Week 11 paths (choose after Friday)

### A — Fundamentals still unsafe or unfinished
- **Monday–Wednesday:** one familiar, approved vegetable/egg or Bistro-relevant production vehicle with deliberate food protection, dish-flow, knife-wash and full-close checks.
- Short explicit safety correction; no new technique simply to meet the pacing guide.
- Evidence target: students demonstrate corrected procedure, not just recite it.

### B — Safety stable, heat adjustment weak
- **Monday:** pan preheat and visual/sound cues with simple vegetables.
- **Tuesday:** sweat versus sauté / browning versus steaming using the same vegetables; students explain and adjust heat.
- **Wednesday:** repeat in a connected recipe with independent planning and a full close.
- **Thursday–Friday:** Bistro Prep/Service and observe transfer.
- Defer the planned roasting emphasis until students can explain and adjust pan heat.

### C — Safety stable, heat adjustment demonstrated
- **Monday:** oven safety, rack/tray spacing and heat communication.
- **Tuesday:** roasted vegetables; same cut/size and spacing controls; compare results.
- **Wednesday:** repeat roasting with students planning timing and judging doneness beyond the timer.
- **Thursday–Friday:** Bistro Prep/Service, approved oven application where genuinely needed.
- One central target: control heat and judge results; avoid adding unrelated products.

## Weekly planning automation rule

Before generating a new DTG, inspect (1) the latest active or approved working DTGs and instructor decisions, (2) current GitHub WACOS standards and this depth map, and (3) dated pacing. Distinguish **planned / taught / coached / independent / not verified**. Never infer student mastery from a completed lesson or recipe. Ask for a concise instructor evidence update when no reliable performance record exists. Propose a *smallest useful repeat* rather than a novel recipe; request instructor approval before generating final printables, quantities or grocery lists.

## Friday evidence prompt for instructor

**What did students actually do independently?** Which of food protection, dishes/air drying, knife safety, heat adjustment and full close still required adult rescue? Was any safety problem unresolved? Choose Path A, B or C only after these observations.
