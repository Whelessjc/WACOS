# Culinary 2 - Week 1 Teaching Guide and Actual Implementation Record

> Canonical live guide and actual implementation record for August 11-14, 2026. The older day-labeled DTGs preserve the original plan as supporting historical sources; they are not evidence that every planned activity occurred.

## Weekly Destination

- **Course:** Culinary 2
- **Unit:** Re-entry, Trust, and Baseline
- **Weekly Anchor:** Students re-enter the Culinary 2 operating system, show current knowledge, rebuild knife fundamentals with demonstration and coaching, complete sautéed vegetables over rice, and leave the kitchen reset.
- **Actual learning sequence:** Re-entry -> knowledge baseline -> knife demonstration/reteach and baseline -> sautéed vegetables over rice.
- **Knowledge:** Current routines, recipe and production reasoning, knife-safety principles, rice method, sauté cues, and reset expectations.
- **Skill:** Explanation, safe setup, controlled knife work after reteach, mise en place, rice cookery, sautéing, tasting, communication, and reset.
- **Professional thinking:** Readiness, humility about retained skill, response to coaching, reliable execution, and follow-through.

## Actual Implementation Notes

- Students needed a knife demonstration/reteach before independent knife work. Prior Culinary 1 completion was not treated as proof of retained technique.
- The planned standalone Bistro and sanitation readiness circuit did not occur and must not be recorded as completed.
- Its useful checks—sanitizer verification, labels/FIFO, thermometer calibration, dish flow, storage, towel use, and reset—should be embedded into authentic production as those systems are used.
- The specific Week 1 dish was **sautéed vegetables over rice**. Future Culinary 2 planning should name the instructional dish when known rather than use vague wording such as “simple production.”

## Show / Students Do

| Sequence | Instructor shows or explains | Students do |
|---|---|---|
| Re-entry | Culinary 2 purpose, current expectations, operating rhythm, and reset | Re-enter routines and identify what must be rebuilt |
| Knowledge baseline | Current prompts and expectations without assuming retained mastery | Demonstrate or explain current knowledge; reveal gaps for reteach |
| Knife reteach/baseline | Stable board, grip, claw, cut path, pace, scrap control, and usable yield | Practice only after demonstration and authorization; respond to coaching and complete knife-station reset |
| Sautéed vegetables over rice | Rice sequence, sauté heat cues, tasting, timing, sanitation checks, and shutdown | Read and prepare, cook rice, sauté vegetables, taste, plate, record actual yield, and reset |

## Must Happen - Core Learning

- Re-establish expectations and gather a meaningful knowledge baseline.
- Demonstrate/reteach knife fundamentals before independent cutting.
- Require instructor authorization and controlled pace for knife and heat work.
- Produce sautéed vegetables over rice using the marked group recipe and actual quality cues.
- Embed the sanitation and readiness checks that authentic production makes relevant.
- Complete storage, labels, dishes, tool return, towels, trash, floors, equipment shutdown, and instructor-approved reset.

## If Time Remains - Flexible Learning

- Extend the knowledge baseline through short line checks or demonstrations.
- Add another controlled cut only after safe control is visible.
- Compare how cut size affects sauté timing and doneness.
- Give ready groups more ownership of timing or a purposeful seasoning adjustment.

## If Time Is Lost

- Protect the knowledge baseline, knife demonstration/reteach, safe controlled practice, named dish, and reset.
- Reduce the number of cuts or vegetable variety.
- Use pre-portioned ingredients or instructor-prepared rice when required.
- Move sanitizer, thermometer, label/FIFO, dish-flow, storage, or towel checks to the next authentic production encounter instead of recreating a standalone circuit.

## Clean Stopping Points

- After a complete re-entry or baseline prompt set.
- After the current vegetable is complete, the knife is secured, product/scraps are routed, and the station is reset.
- After rice or sauté reaches a safe completed stage and the instructor makes the hold/store/discard decision.
- Reset is required before dismissal.

## Materials and Adult Roles

- Use the [Sautéed Vegetables over Rice group recipe](Student_Materials/Day_01_Vegetable_Rice_Bowl_Recipe_and_Mise_Plan.md) and the existing knife observation record.
- The former readiness checklist is historical reference only; use current posted procedures, product directions, test strips, and instructor confirmation during authentic work.
- **Instructor:** demonstrates/reteaches, authorizes knives/equipment/heat, controls safety and allergens, coaches corrections, and approves reset.
- **TA:** observes assigned students/stations, records concise evidence, prompts planning and communication, supports dish/reset flow, and escalates concerns without authorizing equipment or making final judgments.
