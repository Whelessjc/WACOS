# Day 4 Daily Teaching Guide: Vegetable Rice Bowl Baseline Lab

- **Course:** Culinary 2
- **Unit:** Re-entry, Trust, and Baseline
- **Week/day:** Week 1, Day 4 - Friday, August 14, 2026
- **Weekly Anchor:** Students can re-enter the Culinary 2 operating system, read and plan before acting, demonstrate retained knife and sanitation fundamentals, complete a simple lab with improving independence, and leave the kitchen ready for the next crew.

## Learning

- **Target:** I can execute a simple vegetable rice bowl with controlled heat, purposeful tasting, clear communication, and an independent reset.
- **Essential question:** What does a simple product reveal about kitchen usefulness?
- **Knowledge:** Recipe sequence, rice timing, saute heat cues, seasoning checkpoints, quality standards, holding, and shutdown.
- **Skill:** Mise en place, rice cookery, vegetable sauteing, tasting, timing, plating, communication, and reset.
- **Professional thinking:** Reliable execution, composure, correction, and follow-through.

## Opening

Retrieve the marked recipe, confirm rotated group roles, post the first three actions, and name today's flames-off time.

## Core Learning

1. Begin with recipe, role, allergy, equipment, and station checks before ingredients or heat.
2. Require groups to state the stovetop rice method, vegetable plan, heat cues, quality target, and shutdown time.
3. Authorize equipment and heat only after mise en place and the sanitation system are ready.
4. Cook rice using the approved stovetop method: bring to a boil, stir once, cover, reduce to low, cook 15 minutes, remove from heat, and rest covered 10 minutes before fluffing. Saute vegetables with enough pan heat to cook without uncontrolled burning or prolonged steaming.
5. Taste before service and make one purposeful seasoning adjustment with instructor-approved ingredients.
6. Plate three consistent portions, identify one foodways/place connection if useful, and complete a brief quality check.
7. Record actual yield and any instructor-approved deviation on the group recipe.
8. Complete storage, labels, dishes, tool return, towels, trash, floors, equipment shutdown, and teacher sign-off.
9. Conduct a short oral reflection using the lab reference; collect written evidence only when needed.

## Flexible Learning

- Use pre-portioned ingredients or instructor-prepared rice when a section needs a shorter baseline.
- Reduce the vegetable mix while protecting heat control, tasting, and reset.
- Ask ready groups to manage timing with fewer instructor prompts or compare how cut size affected cooking.
- Stop production at a complete stage if safety, equipment, or time requires it; protect the reset.

**Clean stopping point:** complete the current rice or saute stage, make a safe hold/discard decision, turn heat off, and reset.

## Closure

What evidence from today shows that your group can be trusted with more responsibility, and what must still improve?

## Materials and Instructor Preparation

- Each group's marked Vegetable Rice Bowl Recipe and Mise Plan.
- One reusable Vegetable Rice Bowl Lab and Reflection per group.
- Rice, water, safely stored Wednesday vegetables, oil, salt, pepper, and any approved acid or herb for adjustment.
- Lidded stovetop pots, saute pans, knives and boards for authorized students if additional cutting is needed, bowls, measuring tools, spoons, spatulas, tasting utensils, plates or bowls, and storage/labeling supplies.
- Verify Wednesday vegetables are correctly labeled, refrigerated, and suitable for use. Confirm ingredient quantities, equipment condition, group size, allergen plan, and food-distribution rule.

## Adult Roles

**Instructor:** Approve recipe or substitutions, control equipment and heat authorization, make safety and allergen decisions, coach critical corrections, and approve final reset.

**TA when available:** Observe assigned students or stations, prompt planning and communication, support dish flow and reset, and record evidence without independently authorizing equipment or making final safety decisions.

## Students Will Ask

- **Can we change the vegetables or seasoning?** Only within the instructor-approved options and after the group can explain the effect on timing or quality.
- **How do we know the pan is ready?** Use the instructor-confirmed visual, sound, and heat cues; ask before guessing.
- **What if our rice or vegetables are not ready together?** Communicate early, protect quality, and use the instructor-approved recovery plan.
- **Can food leave the classroom?** Food remains in the classroom unless the instructor announces a school-approved exception before service.
- **Are we graded on being perfect?** No. The baseline records evidence of planning, safety, technique, communication, correction, and reset.

## Instructor Context

The simple product is intentional. It makes student habits visible without hiding them behind recipe complexity. Use the evidence to set Week 2 grouping, supports, and independence.
