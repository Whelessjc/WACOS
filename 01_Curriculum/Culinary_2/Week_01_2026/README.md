# Culinary 2 Week 1 - August 11-14, 2026

Week 1 contains four instructional days. This page records the actual implementation and points to one canonical weekly guide for Culinary 2 paths 5-8.

## Weekly Anchor

Students re-enter the Culinary 2 operating system, show current knowledge, rebuild knife fundamentals with demonstration and coaching, complete sautéed vegetables over rice, and leave the kitchen ready for the next crew.
