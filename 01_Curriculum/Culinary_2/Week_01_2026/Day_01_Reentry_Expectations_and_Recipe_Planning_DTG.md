# Day 1 Daily Teaching Guide: Re-entry, Expectations, and Recipe Planning

> **Supporting historical plan.** Active navigation now uses `Culinary_2_Week_1_Teaching_Guide.md`. Actual implementation included re-entry followed by a knowledge baseline; do not treat every planned step below as completed.

- **Course:** Culinary 2
- **Unit:** Re-entry, Trust, and Baseline
- **Week/day:** Week 1, Day 1 - Tuesday, August 11, 2026
- **Weekly Anchor:** Students can re-enter the Culinary 2 operating system, read and plan before acting, demonstrate retained knife and sanitation fundamentals, complete a simple lab with improving independence, and leave the kitchen ready for the next crew.

## Learning

- **Target:** I can explain the Culinary 2 standard, mark a recipe before cooking, and build a usable mise en place plan.
- **Essential question:** What does it mean to be trusted with more in Culinary 2?
- **Knowledge:** Black coat expectations, daily rhythm, flames-off standard, recipe features, group roles, and quality cues.
- **Skill:** Reading, marking, sequencing, planning, and professional communication.
- **Professional thinking:** Readiness, ownership, and composure.

## Opening

Write one kitchen habit you retained from Culinary 1 and one habit you need to rebuild.

## Core Learning

1. Welcome students back and frame Culinary 2 as deeper fundamentals, higher expectations, and greater independence rather than constant novelty.
2. Review the black coat as a symbol of readiness and the course north star: useful without constant supervision.
3. Walk the daily operating rhythm from entry through reset. Re-establish flames off 15 minutes before the bell.
4. Give each group the Vegetable Rice Bowl Recipe and Mise Plan. Read it once without touching equipment.
5. Mark planned yield, time, equipment, hazards, action verbs, quality cues, and shutdown needs.
6. Assign lead cook, mise cook, and sanitation/service cook for the planning rehearsal. Roles rotate Friday.
7. Build the group mise list and name the first three actions before ending.

## Flexible Learning

- Complete a short room and station walk for students who need reorientation.
- Ask groups to identify one place or ingredient connection in the dish without turning the discussion into a lecture.
- Use Culinary 2 Line Check cards DS1-DS5 and RR1-RR5.
- If time is short, protect the recipe read, first three actions, hazards, and shutdown plan.

**Clean stopping point:** every group can explain its recipe, roles, first three actions, and shutdown time.

## Closure

What is one decision a trusted Culinary 2 student should make before heat begins?

## Materials and Instructor Preparation

- One Vegetable Rice Bowl Recipe and Mise Plan per group.
- Active projection source or posted agenda, target, and shutdown time.
- Confirm group size, Friday ingredients, available burners and lidded pots, allergens, and food-distribution rule. The approved rice method is stovetop.
- Prepare provisional groups while allowing for schedule changes through Day 10.

## Adult Roles

**Instructor:** Establish course purpose, confirm non-negotiables, model concise recipe marking, and retain all safety and equipment authority.

**TA when available:** Learn names and groups, question students about sequence rather than supplying answers, and record recurring gaps in recipe literacy or daily systems.

## Students Will Ask

- **Are we cooking today?** No. Today proves that Culinary 2 students plan before they act.
- **Why are we reviewing Culinary 1 skills?** Culinary 2 begins by verifying fundamentals so independence is earned rather than assumed.
- **Can we change Friday's recipe?** Only if the instructor approves a substitution that preserves the week's skill targets.
- **Do roles stay the same?** No. Roles rotate so every student remains responsible for planning, production, and reset.

## Instructor Context

Students may remember the vocabulary but not consistently apply it. Require evidence through action and explanation; do not treat confident speech as proof of readiness.
