# Day 3 Daily Teaching Guide: Bistro and Sanitation Readiness

- **Course:** Culinary 2
- **Unit:** Re-entry, Trust, and Baseline
- **Week/day:** Week 1, Day 3 - Thursday, August 13, 2026
- **Weekly Anchor:** Students can re-enter the Culinary 2 operating system, read and plan before acting, demonstrate retained knife and sanitation fundamentals, complete a simple lab with improving independence, and leave the kitchen ready for the next crew.

## Learning

- **Target:** I can demonstrate the sanitation and Bistro systems that a trusted Culinary 2 student should retain.
- **Essential question:** How do systems protect the next person, product, and service?
- **Knowledge:** Hand-sink readiness, three-compartment sink order, sanitizer concentration, thermometer calibration, thawing, labeling, FIFO, dish flow, storage, towel use, and grease-fire response.
- **Skill:** Demonstrating, checking, explaining, labeling, calibrating, and correcting a system.
- **Professional thinking:** Reliability, verification, stewardship, and early communication.

## Opening

Which kitchen system would create the greatest risk if everyone merely assumed it was ready?

## Core Learning

1. Explain that today's work verifies retained systems; it is not a lecture tour.
2. Assign groups to readiness stations using the Bistro and Sanitation Readiness Check.
3. Require students to demonstrate hand-sink readiness and three-compartment sink wash/rinse/sanitize order.
4. Have students mix or verify sanitizer using the approved product directions and read a test strip; the instructor confirms the required range.
5. Demonstrate thermometer calibration with an ice bath and explain what to do when a thermometer is inaccurate.
6. Check approved thawing methods and complete a full practice label: product, preparation information, discard/use-by information as required, class/group or initials, and FIFO placement.
7. Walk Bistro storage, dish flow, towel routing, restricted areas, and extinguisher location. Review grease-fire response; students point and explain rather than operate emergency equipment.
8. Correct failures and require the group to demonstrate the corrected system before moving on.
9. Verify that Wednesday's labeled vegetables remain safely stored and suitable for Friday; the instructor decides whether any questionable product is discarded.

## Flexible Learning

- Reduce the number of simultaneous stations when supervision is limited.
- Ask ready students to identify why a system exists and what failure it prevents.
- Use Line Check cards SS1, SS4, SS5, CKC2, and CKC3.
- If time is short, protect sanitizer verification, thermometer calibration, labeling, and fire response.

**Clean stopping point:** finish and reset the current readiness station before rotating or dismissing.

## Closure

Name one system you demonstrated and the evidence that proved it was correct.

## Materials and Instructor Preparation

- Reusable Bistro and Sanitation Readiness Check per group or station.
- Approved sanitizer, test strips, buckets or sink setup, thermometer, ice, water, labels, markers, sample container, towels, and appropriate PPE.
- Confirm current manufacturer directions, required sanitizer range, label convention, chemical handling expectations, and restricted areas before class.
- Use simulated food or containers for thawing and labeling scenarios when real product is unnecessary.

## Adult Roles

**Instructor:** Confirm all safety-critical standards, control chemical use, approve corrections, and retain authority for sanitizer, equipment, allergen, and fire-safety guidance.

**TA when available:** Manage an assigned non-authorizing observation station, prompt students to demonstrate rather than recite, record gaps, and escalate any unsafe or uncertain response immediately.

## Students Will Ask

- **Why do we have to demonstrate this again?** Culinary 2 responsibility is based on current evidence, not last year's memory.
- **What sanitizer concentration is correct?** Use the posted range and approved product directions confirmed by the instructor; do not rely on memory alone.
- **Do we use an inaccurate thermometer?** Follow the instructor's calibration and replacement procedure before using it for food decisions.
- **Are we opening the Bistro Friday?** No. Week 1 is readiness and baseline work; there is no public service.

## Instructor Context

The weekly guide intentionally names these procedures because Culinary 2 previously assumed retention without verifying it. Record class-wide gaps so Week 2 can reteach them deliberately.
