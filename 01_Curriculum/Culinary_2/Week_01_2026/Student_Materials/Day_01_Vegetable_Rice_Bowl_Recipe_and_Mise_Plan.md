# Vegetable Rice Bowl - Week 1 Group Recipe and Mise Plan

**Culinary 2 | Tuesday planning and Friday baseline lab**

**Planned yield:** 3 portions  
**Actual yield:** __________ portions

## Ingredients

- 1 cup long-grain rice
- 2 cups water
- 1/2 teaspoon salt for the rice, or instructor-approved amount
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil, divided as needed
- 1/2 onion
- 1 carrot
- 1 celery stalk
- 1/2 bell pepper
- Salt and pepper for adjustment, as approved
- Optional instructor-approved acid or fresh herb for final adjustment

## Method

1. Read the complete recipe, confirm group roles, and post the flames-off time.
2. Wash hands and prepare the approved sanitation system.
3. Gather equipment and ingredients. Complete mise en place before heat.
4. Rinse or do not rinse the rice according to the instructor's direction.
5. Combine the rice, 2 cups water, and 1/2 teaspoon salt in a lidded stovetop pot.
6. Bring to a boil. Stir once, cover, reduce the heat to low, and cook for 15 minutes. Do not lift the lid.
7. Remove the pot from heat and rest, covered, for 10 minutes. Fluff with a fork.
8. Retrieve Wednesday's labeled vegetables after instructor approval. Complete any additional cutting only with authorization.
9. Heat a saute pan over medium to medium-high heat as directed. Add the oil, then add the onion, carrot, celery, and bell pepper.
10. Saute for approximately 5-8 minutes, stirring as needed, until the vegetables are tender but retain color and texture. Watch sound, steam, color, and pan temperature; communicate before the product is at risk.
11. Taste with an approved utensil. Make one purposeful seasoning adjustment.
12. Portion rice and vegetables into three consistent bowls. Complete the quality check.
13. Record actual yield and any approved recipe change.
14. Store or discard food as directed and complete the full station reset.
