# Vegetable Rice Bowl Baseline Lab and Reflection

**Culinary 2 - Day 4 | Reusable group reference**

Use during the lab. Complete the reflection orally unless the instructor requests written evidence.

## Before Heat

- [ ] Group roles rotated and understood
- [ ] Marked recipe read completely
- [ ] First three actions and shutdown time posted
- [ ] Hands washed and sanitation system ready
- [ ] Equipment and ingredients checked
- [ ] Rice method and vegetable cut plan confirmed
- [ ] Hazards, allergens, and approval points identified
- [ ] Instructor authorization received

## During Production

- [ ] Rice timing monitored
- [ ] Vegetable cut size supports even cooking
- [ ] Pan heat checked through sound, steam, color, and response
- [ ] Group communicates before timing or quality becomes a crisis
- [ ] Product tasted with an approved utensil
- [ ] One purposeful seasoning adjustment made
- [ ] Flames-off time protected

## Product and Evidence

- [ ] Three consistent portions presented
- [ ] Rice texture checked
- [ ] Vegetable color, texture, and doneness checked
- [ ] Final seasoning checked
- [ ] Actual yield and approved deviations recorded

## Reset

- [ ] Food stored, labeled, or discarded as directed
- [ ] Heat and equipment off
- [ ] Dishes routed correctly
- [ ] Counters cleaned and sanitized
- [ ] Tools returned and towels routed
- [ ] Trash and floors checked
- [ ] Instructor reset sign-off complete
