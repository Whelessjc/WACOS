# Day 2 Daily Teaching Guide: Knife Control Baseline

> **Supporting historical plan.** Active navigation now uses `Culinary_2_Week_1_Teaching_Guide.md`. Actual implementation required an instructor knife demonstration/reteach before independent baseline work.

- **Course:** Culinary 2
- **Unit:** Re-entry, Trust, and Baseline
- **Week/day:** Week 1, Day 2 - Wednesday, August 12, 2026
- **Weekly Anchor:** Students can re-enter the Culinary 2 operating system, read and plan before acting, demonstrate retained knife and sanitation fundamentals, complete a simple lab with improving independence, and leave the kitchen ready for the next crew.

## Learning

- **Target:** I can set a safe board, produce controlled cuts, protect usable yield, and reset without waiting for every direction.
- **Essential question:** What does knife control reveal about readiness?
- **Knowledge:** Board stability, grip, claw, cut-size purpose, scrap control, usable yield, and knife sanitation.
- **Skill:** Controlled slicing and dicing with consistent size and a safe pace.
- **Professional thinking:** Precision before speed, awareness, and responsible use of product.

## Opening

From memory, sketch or list everything that belongs in a safe knife station before the knife moves.

## Core Learning

1. Inspect student setup before distributing or authorizing knives.
2. Use a brief callback demonstration: stable board, grip, claw, clear cut path, and scrap container.
3. Students produce instructor-selected cuts using onion, carrot, celery, and/or bell pepper.
4. Observe control first, then consistency, usable yield, station organization, and response to correction.
5. Stop any student whose pace exceeds control; assign a safer production or observation role until the instructor authorizes return.
6. Sort usable product and waste as directed. Place usable vegetables in approved containers, label completely, refrigerate promptly, and carry them forward for Friday under the instructor's food-safety procedures.
7. Complete knife return, board sanitation, scrap handling, floor check, and full reset.

## Flexible Learning

- Require one controlled cut rather than several styles when students need rebuilding.
- Ask ready students to compare cut sizes and explain their effect on Friday's cooking time.
- Add yield or timing evidence only after safe control is established.
- Use Line Check cards KC1-KC5 while students wait for inspection.

**Clean stopping point:** complete the current vegetable, secure the knife, sort product, and reset the station.

## Closure

Name one piece of evidence that showed control and one correction you will carry into Friday.

## Materials and Instructor Preparation

- Knives, cutting boards, damp towels or approved board-stability method, scrap containers, sheet pans or storage containers, labels, sanitizer, and cleaning supplies.
- Instructor-selected onions, carrots, celery, and/or bell peppers.
- Knife Control Baseline Record for instructor/TA evidence.
- Prepare approved containers and refrigeration space for Wednesday vegetables that will carry forward to Friday. Confirm the required label information and cooling/storage procedure before class.

## Adult Roles

**Instructor:** Authorize knife access, model only the necessary callback, make safety interventions, and determine whether each student's evidence supports Friday participation.

**TA when available:** Observe only assigned students or stations using the baseline record, capture concise evidence, monitor scraps and reset, and immediately escalate unsafe movement. The TA does not authorize knife use.

## Students Will Ask

- **Why are we doing basic cuts again?** Culinary 2 verifies control before adding speed, pressure, or production volume.
- **Does speed count?** Only after safe, consistent control is visible.
- **Are we saving these vegetables?** Yes. Usable vegetables are placed in approved containers, fully labeled, refrigerated promptly, and used Friday. Waste and unsuitable product are discarded as directed.
- **What if I am not cleared for a knife today?** Complete the assigned safer role and use feedback to prepare for the next opportunity.

## Instructor Context

This is a diagnostic baseline, not a surprise practical or a permanent judgment. Evidence should shape grouping, Friday roles, and the next instructional steps.
