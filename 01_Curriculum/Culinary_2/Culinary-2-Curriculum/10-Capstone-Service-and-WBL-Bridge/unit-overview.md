## Learning Priorities

- Execute a meaningful practical task or service role.
- Plan prep and timing.
- Communicate under pressure.
- Maintain sanitation and reset standards.
- Reflect on performance using evidence.
- Connect kitchen behavior to workplace readiness.

## Confirmed Capstone Format

Five-course tasting menu for Explore Charleston guests, with courses informed by actual visiting chefs. Plan/test in Weeks 29–31, rehearse the whole operation in Week 32, prefer Week 33 execution and protect Week 34 as emergency buffer. Actual roster and guest availability remain open. Observe individual planning, execution, communication, maintenance and closing within the team event; a plated component alone is incomplete evidence. Other practical checks support this capstone rather than replacing the confirmed format without a new instructor decision.

## Evidence

Students should be able to answer:

- What was the plan?
- What changed?
- What did you adjust?
- How did you communicate?
- What would you do next time?

## Build-Out Notes

Build the near-term five-course plan from actual chef notes and reviewed recipes; use current assessment tools and short useful planning records rather than a new packet of forms.
