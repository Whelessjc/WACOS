# Annual Adjustment Process

This curriculum is meant to be maintained year to year.

Do not rebuild it from scratch unless the course identity changes.

## Before the Year Starts

Update:

- school calendar
- testing windows
- early release dates
- known field trips
- guest speakers
- pep rallies or major school events
- Hospitality Scholars Tuesdays
- Bistro start date
- CKC target window

Then choose:

- anchor recipes for each module
- Bistro opening menu
- likely Semester 2 baking products
- capstone format options

## Quarter Review Routine

At the end of each quarter, answer:

- Which routines held?
- Which routines drifted?
- Which skill needed more reps?
- Which recipe helped?
- Which recipe wasted time?
- What did Bistro expose?
- What should move earlier next year?
- What can be cut?

Keep notes short.

## What to Protect Every Year

Protect:

- stable beginning routine
- stable ending routine
- Thursday Bistro prep
- Friday Bistro service
- knife control
- recipe reading
- clean-as-you-go
- tasting and seasoning
- communication
- station reset
- composure under pressure

## What Can Change

Change:

- recipes
- Bistro menu
- capstone format
- exact week placement
- number of reps per skill
- assessment check dates
- Semester 2 module order

## CKC Review

After the CKC window, note:

- Was Week 9 realistic?
- Which CKC skills were strong?
- Which skills needed more kitchen evidence?
- Did students understand the credential as part of the kitchen work?
- What line checks should be added earlier?

## Bistro Review

After each month of Bistro, note:

- strongest station
- weakest station
- recurring prep issue
- recurring communication issue
- product quality issue
- sanitation issue
- one change for next month

## 2027-2028 Opening Development

The 2027-2028 Culinary 2 opening is a proposed annual redesign, not current policy. Do not assume that students enter with an equally retained Culinary 1 foundation.

During the 2026-2027 reset, collect concise evidence about which fundamentals students retain, which require demonstration or repetition, how quickly students regain independence, and which drills or simple production tasks transfer into later work. Use that evidence to refine a proposed two- to four-week common-baseline opening covering:

- Culinary 1 fundamentals and Kitchen Mode
- recipe and prep-list reading
- mise en place, workflow, and cleaning systems
- communication and sensory awareness
- purposeful movement, scanning, and follow-through
- sanitation verification and practical readiness

Review the evidence in spring 2027 before adopting the design. If approved, build a dated 2027-2028 opening package and revise the annual weekly pacing. Preserve the Culinary 2 course identity and broader framework unless a separate program decision changes them. WACOS remains the continuing source of truth; the new school year receives dated implementation materials rather than a separate repository.

## End-of-Year Reset

At the end of the year:

1. Save the working version.
2. Mark recipes as keep, revise, or replace.
3. Update line check cards.
4. Update Bistro systems.
5. Update Semester 1 pacing notes.
6. Review the 2027-2028 common-baseline evidence and proposed opening decision.
7. Leave clear notes for next year's start.

Do not over-polish the documents. They should stay usable in a real culinary lab.
