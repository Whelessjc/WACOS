# Culinary 1 Spring 2027 Implementation Decision

Adopted November 3, 2026 by instructor approval of the spring proposal, followed by same-session event and year-end refinements. Applies to the new spring Culinary 1 cohort only.

## Approved direction

Use the October 17 fundamentals redesign: early useful cooking, prerequisites before first use, individual recipe comprehension and short workflow plans, familiar recipes repeated with correction, station maintenance and complete reset. Weeks 1–3 build foundations; Week 4 is training/rehearsal only; Week 5 is the readiness-dependent public-launch target. Thereafter protect Monday–Wednesday learning, Thursday Prep and Friday Service through the final regular Bistro window.

- Week 9 Wednesday: dedicated end-of-quarter deep clean, individual zones, inspection and correction; replace cooking and retain Thursday Prep/Friday Service.
- Spring C1 Chopped moves to Week 14, April 12–14. Preserve existing house rules, progressive elimination, 5-minute planning, 30-minute cooking, two identical plates and complete cleaning before results. This does not automatically change C2 Chopped. Resolve the existing judging/tie-handling implementation before use.
- Final regular Bistro remains the preferred April 23 window, subject to readiness and program decisions.
- Thursday April 29, Week 16: Teacher Appreciation meal. Use the fall large-scale Korean-beef meal as the operational model with a different menu. Do not inherit the 200-guest count. Tuesday plan/rehearse; Wednesday approved production; Thursday service; Friday close/review. Monday April 26 remains a closure unless activated for weather makeup.
- Preserve familiar cleaning, production/handoff and service in the spring final. Accept event evidence only where individually observed and instructor accepted; do not create assessment weights.
- May 21 is the expected last day with students. May 17–21 supports reflection, breakdown, cleaning, inventory, needed demonstrations and next-year planning. May 24–27 is the uncommon makeup window; no new required learning depends on attendance. The published May 27 last-student-day and May 28 teacher-workday entries remain calendar facts.

## Resource direction

Build 18 current Prototype E weekly guides, a week-local full-recipe support set and reusable references for common measurements, safe knife skills/cuts, cooking methods/recipe verbs, pots/pans, recipe-to-work planning and maintain/reset/close. Use current standards; keep generated delivery copies outside GitHub under the Artifact Publishing Workflow. Instructor review precedes student use or accepted-artifact publication. Formula approval, source checking and kitchen testing remain separate.

## Operational dependencies

INSTRUCTOR DECISION REQUIRED: Confirm the April 29 menu, guest count/portion, service method/time and safe production/holding or cooling plan before food production. Rehearse without food until resolved. The April 29 date is the instructor-selected program date; it does not assert the national observance dates.

Week 13 frying requires a selected product, complete recipe, current equipment SOP and individual authorization; retain an approved familiar pan/oven fallback. Live Bistro menus/builds and detailed testing interruptions are confirmed near use. No new policy, equipment permission, credential, menu, grade conversion or cash-handling rule is adopted here.
