# October 2, 2026 — Independence, Station Readiness, and Released from Duties

## Classification

**Program Decision**

## Decision

West Ashley Culinary will preserve a kitchen environment that is safe for questions while explicitly building student independence, investigation, communication, station readiness, and responsibility through completion.

### Bistro Jobs

The student Bistro jobs are:

- Service
- Barista
- Line Cooks
- Support
- Expo / Floaters

**KP refers to the instructor's assistant / Teaching Assistant and is not a student Bistro job.**

### Not Kitchen Ready vs Released from Duties

**Not Kitchen Ready** is the entry/readiness gate before practical kitchen work begins.

**Released from Duties** applies after a student has entered practical work but continued participation becomes inappropriate because of behavior during class or lab. Examples may include leaving the assigned class/kitchen area without permission, refusal to follow a direct instruction, or unsafe conduct.

The student completes the current Released from Duties form/reflection before returning to the privilege of kitchen-lab participation. Instructor judgment governs release and return. Discipline, parent contact, Employability evidence, missed-lab documentation, and other school responses remain separate decisions.

### Independent Investigation

The kitchen remains safe for questions. Students are also expected to investigate routine operational questions before escalating them to an adult.

Use:

**Look where it should be. Then look where it could be.**

For an item, ingredient, or tool, students first check the expected location, then think through likely locations based on the item's work cycle or common student misplacement. When appropriate, they communicate with a nearby teammate before asking an adult.

Questions involving safety, allergens, uncertain food handling, or equipment authorization go to an adult immediately.

The goal is not fewer questions. The goal is stronger investigation, communication, and better questions.

### Station Opening

Station opening checklists are operating gates. Students verify that the station is ready before cooking, drink production, or ticket work begins.

### Productive Pauses

A pause in ticket flow is recovery and readiness time, not idle time.

Use:

**Reset → Wipe → Sweep → Check Mise/Product → Restock → Look Ahead**

### Completion and Release

Students remain engaged until released. An individual task being finished does not mean the student's work is finished.

Use:

**The work is finished when the instructor/Teaching Assistant releases the student.**

## Rationale

Recent Bistro Prep and Service evidence showed that student questions about routine locations were consuming instructor bandwidth, station-opening checklists were being skipped, students were treating pauses in tickets as downtime, and some students were ending work based on completion of an individual task rather than the readiness of the operation.

These clarifications support the governing statement:

**We are not training chefs. We are training students to think like chefs.**

They protect both psychological safety and the expectation that students grow in independence, communication, judgment, and situational awareness.
