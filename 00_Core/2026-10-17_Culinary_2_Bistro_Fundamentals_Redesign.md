# Culinary 2 Bistro Fundamentals Redesign

**Lane:** Program Decision. **Status:** Instructor-approved direction and implementation. **Date:** October 17, 2026.
**Source:** Instructor’s course-redesign directions and “proceed,” including West Ashley 25, full-year variety, pasta/wok techniques, student favorites, seasonal hospitality and flexibility.

## Decision

Design the full-year Culinary 2 course around useful work in the actual kitchen and Bistro, growing responsibility and the complete work cycle. Keep fundamentals under pressure while deliberately including regional identity, student enjoyment, international technique and real school/community events. Use the [Modular Pacing Guide](../01_Curriculum/Culinary_2/01_Modular_Pacing_Guide.md) as the single governing year sequence. The West Ashley 25 supplies recurring applications throughout the year, not a separate unit or a mandatory 25-dish checklist. Events replace planned applications; preserve capacity for repetitions and unexpected requests.

## Implementation

- Three-week opening bootcamp; prerequisite measuring, reading, equipment, safety, dish/reset and technical instruction before first use. Model realistic sequences and checkpoints; prior experience is uneven.
- Week 4 training/mock only; normal Week 5 readiness-based public opening. Spring C1 Week 4 also remains training; shared opening normally targets overall Week 23. C2-only service requires instructor authorization and adequate staffing.
- Keep the normal Monday–Wednesday instructional space and Thursday Prep/Friday Service. Stop cooking at least 15 minutes before the bell unless an earlier clean stop is needed.
- Repeat regional rice, vegetables, seafood, barbecue, cold-station and baked applications across the year. Include chicken/shrimp Alfredo, pasta cookery and authorized wok use; fun is a valid instructional purpose.
- Plan Halloween, Thanksgiving feast, faculty holiday meal, Valentine’s chocolates, Pi Day and Teacher Appreciation windows around actual dates, scope and readiness. Superintendent/visitor requests must fit staffing, instruction and a complete close; identify displaced work explicitly.
- Build balanced teams from applications, readiness and need. Support is skilled work; each station cleans its own area. No mandatory rotation or student KP job.
- Progress modeled → coached → reviewed student plan → demonstrated ownership. Retain individual accommodations and access supports; do not infer independence from course label or calendar.
- Use individual evidence of opening, production/service, maintenance and close under current assessment rules; no new deductions, collective grade, automatic course removal or disability-based placement rule. Position review and class-specific service pauses remain readiness decisions with explicit expectations and supervised practice.
- Keep Week 16 Chopped rules exactly. Keep the confirmed five-course Explore Charleston tasting menu: Week 33 preferred, Week 34 emergency buffer, actual roster/date open. Protect end-of-year completion and shutdown capacity.
- Practical math is embedded. Omit standalone history/brigade, sustainability, business-management, formal costing/menu-analysis and nutrition survey units from this chosen sequence. No claim of full state-indicator coverage or waiver of external requirements.
- CKC alignment remains; verify actual credential requirements, registration and test dates separately. No external sourdough program or automatic student ownership of the house starter is adopted.

## Application and records

Apply prospectively to Fall 2026 from Week 11 and to future cohort openings. Do not rewrite completed teaching records. Preserve useful old daily ideas as explicitly superseded source material. Current pacing supersedes old folder-number calendars and prior spring-rotation/reopening/capstone alternatives. Later instructional and recipe artifacts follow the latest adopted format, not older attached reference copies where they conflict.

Remaining implementation work: confirm actual event/partner/testing dates; choose reviewed recipes, affordable quantities and food destinations; build near-term daily guides from live evidence. This decision does not automatically produce all daily packets or certify recipes.
