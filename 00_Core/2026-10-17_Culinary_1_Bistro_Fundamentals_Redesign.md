# Culinary 1 Bistro Fundamentals Redesign

**Lane:** Program Decision. **Status:** Instructor-approved. **Date:** October 17, 2026.
**Source:** Instructor's Week 10 reflection, practical-scope directions and approvals of the revised semester progression, including Week 1 measuring before the first lab.

## Decision

Design Culinary 1 backward from useful work in the actual kitchen and Bistro. Teach the complete cycle: **Get ready → Read and plan → Set up → Produce → Maintain → Store and reset → Review.** Replace the former fixed knife/heat/flavor/protein calendar blocks with repeated relevant applications and increasing student responsibility. The [Modular Pacing Guide](../01_Curriculum/Culinary_1/01_Modular_Pacing_Guide.md) governs the semester sequence; technical-resource folder numbers are not calendar units.

## Implementation

- Teach recipe-specific measuring, recipe reading, equipment selection/authorized use, sanitation, dish staging and reset before the first pancake lab. Demonstrate required measuring tools/units and scale/tare when used. Repeat in Week 2; deepen planning and useful scaling in Week 3.
- Teach knife, heat, protein and equipment safety before first needed use. Later weekly emphases are reinforcement, not permission to delay prerequisite instruction.
- Keep Weeks 1-3 foundations, Week 4 outcome-based assembly/mock service with no public service, and Week 5 the normal readiness-dependent launch target. After launch protect Monday-Wednesday instruction, Thursday Prep and Friday Service. Retain Week 16 Chopped under its existing rules.
- Select engaging, useful, verified recipes and repeat them before adding complexity. No food vehicle in the pacing map creates a new menu requirement or approved formula.
- Keep independent online ServSafe Food Handler primary credential delivery with current access, accommodation/intervention support and practical safety instruction. Credential completion is not proof of practical competence.
- Build balanced teams. Applications inform placement rather than guarantee a preferred job; operational need, demonstrated habits/skill and individual supports guide the instructor. Detailed job practice develops depth; automatic rotation remains retired. Support is skilled work, never punishment or a default holding assignment.
- Teach maintenance during work and inspect reset while correction is possible. Each station owns its area; trained available students receive appropriate assistance assignments. Keep existing assessment records and individual evidence; no new grades, points, collective penalties, release/discipline rules or automatic service/reopening thresholds.
- For Fall 2026, apply the new direction prospectively from Week 11 using accepted DTGs and actual evidence; do not rewrite past teaching as completed under the new sequence. Future cohorts use the revised opening.

## Instructional Scope and External Requirements

Standalone history/brigade, sustainability, business management, formal cost/menu analysis and nutrition units are outside this adopted practical sequence. Practical measurement, scaling, yield and portions remain embedded. The SCDE Hospitality framework includes CAM1/5720 indicators in additional strands; this instructor decision does not claim every indicator is covered or waive external requirements. Instructor/school/district authority determines applicability and any external compliance response. Safety-critical and rare-event instruction remains protected.

Vinaigrette, same-day refrigerated quick pickle, tofu and pan sauce remain available useful applications rather than mandatory standalone coverage. Existing safety/allergen/equipment/storage controls still apply; no canning/fermentation permission is introduced.
