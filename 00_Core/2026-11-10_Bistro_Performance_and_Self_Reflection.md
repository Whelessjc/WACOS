# November 10 2026 Bistro Performance and Self Reflection

## Authority and Scope

Instructor-confirmed current practice and same-session direction to revise the printable rubric/calculator and update WACOS. Applies to Friday Bistro in Culinary 1 and Culinary 2. This is a Program Decision; the instructor has authorized implementation and repository publication.

## Adopted Decision

Use one overall 0–5 performance score in one performance column and a separate self-reflection worth 10 points. Include service and complete reset in overall performance. Retire the October 25 separate 45% Performance / 45% Reset / 10% Reflection structure.

Minimum passing performance is a meaningful share of assigned work, response to coaching, and contribution to a complete reset with appropriate support. Needing help, making mistakes, or working slowly does not by itself establish failure.

The failing boundary is: **After clear directions, appropriate support, and an opportunity to improve, the student repeatedly disengages and leaves significant assigned responsibilities for others to complete.**

Adopt the revised performance conversions: 5 = 100%, 4 = 90%, 3 = 75%, 2 = 50%, 1 = 25%, 0 = 0%. Level 3 is meaningful contribution with coaching; level 2 is repeated disengagement with significant responsibilities left for others; level 1 is minimal demonstrated contribution; level 0 is no demonstrated performance despite an available opportunity. Use the [canonical rubric](../03_Assessment/Tools/Bistro_Service_Performance_Rubric.md) for complete level descriptions and calculator.

For each observed service, Bistro grade out of 100 = (performance conversion × 0.90) + reflection points. Reflection is scored out of 10: contribution/reset evidence 4, evaluation 3, next action 3. An honest reflection can earn full reflection credit even after poor performance. It does not replace practical evidence. Level 2 with full reflection earns 55%; level 1 with full reflection earns 32.5%.

## Boundaries and Remaining Work

The 45/35/20 course categories remain unchanged. This decision settles the Bistro performance conversion and individual-service calculation only. Lab and Employability conversions, verified blank-entry rules, adult-recording-failure verification, aggregation across services, absence/makeup reconciliation, and feedback/conference scheduling remain in D001. Keep missing-work placeholders distinct from earned levels; no automatic baseline-four entries for absence or insufficient observation.

Honor approved supports and instructor judgment. Safety and release/return procedures remain separate. Record the actual work left for others and response to coaching. Avoid automatic duplicate penalties across Bistro and Employability.
