# Bistro Job-Placement Decision

Approved: September 25, 2026

## Decision

West Ashley Culinary retires the mandatory five-position, two-service Bistro rotation and shared ten-service cycle.

Bistro jobs are now **placements, not automatic rotations**.

The operating functions remain:

- Service
- Barista
- Line Cooks
- Support / KP
- Expo / Floaters

Students learn the whole Bistro operating system before and during service, but they do not need to cycle through every job on a fixed calendar.

## Placement Process

Students apply for Bistro jobs. The instructor may use a brief informal interview when useful.

The instructor makes final placements according to Bistro demand and staffing needs first, informed by:

- the student's application;
- demonstrated knowledge;
- readiness;
- reliability;
- skill;
- fit for the work.

A placement may last for the remainder of the course.

## Transfer Process

A student who wants a different Bistro job must apply for a transfer and demonstrate the **knowledge, readiness, understanding, and drive** required for the requested role.

A transfer is not guaranteed. Instructor judgment and Bistro staffing needs govern whether and when a move occurs.

## Governing Principle

**Specialize in your job. Understand the whole operation.**

Students are expected to develop depth and fluency in their assigned job while continuing to understand Life of an Order, station interdependence, safety, sanitation, communication, situational awareness, Service School, Prep systems, reset, and closing.

## KP / Support

KP/Support is a real Bistro job with defined operating responsibilities. It is not a fallback assignment, punishment, or free period.

## Retired Practice

The August 26, 2026 mandatory five-team rotation decision is historical only. The requirement that students remain in a position for two consecutive services and then rotate through a shared ten-service cycle no longer governs current Bistro practice.

## Instructor Authority

The instructor retains final authority over placements, transfers, staffing, readiness, safety, equipment access, student-specific judgments, and operational adjustments.

## Implementation Scope

This decision governs the Core Manual, Decision Register, current Bistro Operations and Rhythms, Life of an Order, current course maps and Bistro integration documents, assessment/observation guidance, AI Quick Reference, and other current materials that previously required automatic Bistro rotation.
