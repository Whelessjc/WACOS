## Confirmed Standards
- Culinary 1 product stewardship decision confirmed 2026-08-22: food produced in Culinary has an instructor-approved purpose and is not automatic student food. The early brownie procedure gives exactly one brownie to each team member, packages the remaining ready-to-eat brownies with gloves under current ServSafe Food Handler guidance, and follows an instructor-directed blend of distribution and storage. Welcoming new teachers is the preferred first distribution application; gratitude, community hospitality, Bistro awareness, and informal marketing are legitimate secondary outcomes. Later recipients remain instructor-chosen.
- ServSafe Food Handler scope consolidation confirmed 2026-08-22: the related safety topic is taught during credential instruction and otherwise handled case by case by the instructor, rather than repeated throughout WACOS curriculum, recipe fields, assessments, operating guidance, or generated copies.

The five current instructional and Bistro teams are **Dining, Barista, Kitchen, Support, and Tournant / Expo**. The same teams carry into service.
