## Confirmed Standards
- Version 1.0 remains historical baseline architecture.
- Version 2.1 is the current cleaned operating manual.
- Raw evidence belongs in the research archive, not the operating repo.
- Student-facing AI content must be reviewed before use.
- Certified Kitchen Cook (CKC) fully replaces ServSafe Manager as Culinary 2's credential pathway. CKC is embedded across Weeks 1-9 with a Week 9 practical push; ServSafe Manager test prep is explicitly listed as something Culinary 2 is not. (Resolves former D004. See `01_Curriculum/Culinary_2/00_Master_Framework.md` and `05_CKC_Alignment.md`.)
- Culinary 2 follows an 8-module full-year pacing arc (`01_Modular_Pacing_Guide.md`). Semester 1 is written day-by-day (`02_Semester_1_Weekly_Guide.md`). Semester 2 has a day-by-day draft in `06_Semester_2_Weekly_Guide.md`, pending instructor confirmation of the items in D005, D009, and D010.
- Guest complaint recovery uses the HEARD method (Hear, Empathize, Apologize, Resolve, Diagnose), replacing the older "listen, apologize, thank, act" language. See `West_Ashley_Culinary_Core_Manual.md` (Guest Recovery), `05_Bistro_Operations.md`, and `04_Bistro_Integration.md` (which also keeps a separate, distinct internal Service Recovery protocol for kitchen/station mistakes - HEARD is specifically for guest-facing complaints).
- **E.P.I.C. (Excellence, Passion, Innovation, Collaboration) is retired and is not current program identity.** It appeared in older dining-room training materials as a mission/values framework but is confirmed no longer in use (2026-07). Do not reintroduce it into Program Identity, Mission/Vision, or any current-facing document.
- **The four Bistro teams/rotations are Dining, Kitchen, Support, and Barista** (resolves former D011). "Management Staff" and "Service Staff" are not current team names - use Dining, Kitchen, Support, Barista consistently across Culinary 1, Culinary 2, and all Bistro documentation.
- Foundational additions confirmed 2026-07-20: Culinary 1 adds dried pasta with basic tomato sauce, basic vinaigrette with simple salad, and a basic pan sauce. Whole-chicken breakdown belongs in Culinary 2, subject to calendar, supervision, sourcing, and budget checks. Fresh pasta, risotto, whole turkey, bivalves, fermentation, and a full pastry/baking program were not added.
- Foundation photo-review additions instructor-approved 2026-07-20: tofu is the required Culinary 1 Unit 10 plant-protein vehicle; a teacher-approved quick pickle is included in Culinary 1 Unit 09 for labeled, refrigerated, same-day use only; poaching is explicit in Culinary 2 Unit 4; and broiler/grill fundamentals are introduced in Culinary 1 and applied in Culinary 2 only after equipment-specific SOP, ventilation/fire-safety, instruction/demo, supervision, and individual-authorization gates are met. Quick pickling does not add canning or fermentation, and tempeh is not a required vehicle. The instructor subsequently confirmed the tofu, poaching, quick-pickle, and broiler/grill recommendations as written.
- Calendar decision approved 2026-07-20 and revised 2026-08-09: the CCSD 2026-2027 Academic Calendar governs dated planning. Week 4 is Bistro systems, training, and mock service only; the soft opening is canceled. The Bistro opens Friday, September 11 in Week 5. Semester 2 and capstone anchors follow `2026-07-20_Calendar_Week_Mapping.md`. Week 36 ends May 14 and is not the final student week; May 17-27 remains a year-end buffer pending detailed testing and graduation calendars.
- Branding decision instructor-confirmed 2026-08-04: "Classroom to Table" is the instructor's separate personal brand/project. It is not the official brand, slogan, identity, operating language, or required communications language of WACOS or West Ashley Culinary. WACOS may naturally address similar ideas, but must not present the phrase as belonging to the school program. "High School, High Standards" remains approved program language.
- Startup operations instructor-confirmed 2026-08-04 and Bistro timing revised 2026-08-09: Vector Solutions training is launch-critical and due as soon as possible, with the exact district deadline requiring verification; student-name Anki cards belong in Week 1 and must use approved systems; the 2026-2027 Teacher Assistant is a support role within instructor authority; and Bistro launch uses Weeks 1-3 readiness, Week 4 training and mock service with no soft opening, and a Friday, September 11 opening in Week 5.
- Structure guardrail instructor-confirmed 2026-08-04: Dining, Kitchen, Support, and Barista remain the only current Bistro teams. WACOS and AI-generated plans must not invent classroom jobs, rotation boards, teams, management roles, or student authority structures without a separate instructor-approved Program Decision.
- Instructor workflow documentation decision approved 2026-07-22: `09_Workflows/` is the instructor-only operating layer connecting WACOS, GitHub, ChatGPT, Codex, and external district systems. It does not create curriculum or policy. The instructor-supplied weekly agenda, checklist, and morning procedures are the primary evidence of current practice unless they conflict with the Core Manual. Monday-Wednesday instruction remains protected, Thursday is Bistro Prep, Friday is Bistro Service, and one documented deep clean is required each quarter.
- Bistro payment and deposit decision approved 2026-07-22: accept cash and checks payable to WAHS; use Square for transaction records and kitchen tickets; attach the detailed Square transactions report to the deposit; complete deposits Friday when time permits or Monday otherwise; record approved PBIS incentives and Bistro coins as named comps rather than gift-card/other tender; and use the backup receipt system during Square outages. District and bookkeeper controls remain governing.
- Quarter grade category names confirmed 2026-07-23: 45% Summative Assessments: Projects, Tests, and Bistro; 35% Formative Assessments and Labs; and 20% Employability Skills. This refines the official category names without changing the previously confirmed 45/35/20 weights.
- Professional Thinking Framework approved 2026-07-23: West Ashley Culinary uses culinary as the vehicle for developing transferable professional habits of mind. "We are not training chefs. We are training students to think like chefs." New and substantively revised units, Daily Teaching Guides, assessments, instructor guidance, and AI prompts should connect knowledge, skill, and professional thinking. Existing materials remain usable and enter alignment through normal revision cycles.
- Assessment observation system approved 2026-08-06: West Ashley Culinary uses concise, evidence-based manager observation systems for Bistro, laboratory, and Employability assessment. The systems prioritize observable performance, natural checkpoints, exception-based notes, repeated evidence, improvement, and instructor judgment within the realities of a working high-school kitchen. Not observed is not failure; serious safety concerns receive separate instructor review; Bistro remains Summative, labs remain Formative Assessments and Labs, and Employability remains 20%.
- Teaching-guide standard adopted 2026-07-24 and simplified 2026-08-15: one canonical weekly teaching guide per course/week is the active instructor-facing layer; older daily DTGs may remain supporting sources. Flex-mod planning targets equivalent weekly learning, section continuation, and clean stopping points rather than identical daily pacing. Culinary 1 uses paths 1-4, Culinary 2 uses paths 5-8, and PACK remains the lunch advisory. First-ten-day guides must be especially elastic.
- First-lab food-distribution guidance confirmed 2026-07-24: no added toppings; takeout containers are not provided and must be purchased when an instructor-approved exception permits food to leave; taking food home is not recommended because of food-safety concerns; and food leaving the classroom is not recommended because other classes generally prohibit food and drinks. Exceptions remain instructor-controlled. Students must be responsible and respectful, avoid greed, follow the receiving classroom's rules, and continue observing food safety.
- Finished-artifact publishing decision approved 2026-08-08: GitHub/WACOS remains the authoritative permanent record; the district-approved Google Drive is the durable library for instructor-reviewed, ready-to-use instructional and operational artifacts; ChatGPT supports planning, drafting, analysis, and artifact creation; and Codex maintains repository consistency, validation, decision history, commits, and GitHub publication. Published Drive copies may be reused without regeneration but cannot silently establish policy. Durable curriculum or operating changes must flow back through the WACOS Update Workflow. Generated delivery binaries remain excluded from GitHub unless a specific documented reason requires them.
- Usability and simplification decisions approved 2026-08-15: weekly learning sequences are the primary live planning unit; daily labels are suggested starting points rather than stopping boundaries; one canonical weekly teaching guide per course/week replaces separate daily DTGs in active navigation; Weeks 1-4 are Monday-Friday readiness/instruction weeks; plain-language naming governs Drive artifacts and visible navigation while stable repository filenames remain unless misleading; Teacher + TA debriefs are brief and sustainable with no required duration or new form; and physical capture follows NOTICE -> CAPTURE -> PROCESS -> ROUTE -> CONFIRM.
- Recipe preservation decision approved 2026-08-15: existing Recipe Bank Word exports remain as a documented preservation snapshot, Markdown is the maintained source, and new delivery binaries are not added to GitHub by default.
