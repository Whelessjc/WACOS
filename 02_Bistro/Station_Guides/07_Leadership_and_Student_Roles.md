# Leadership and Student Roles

## Purpose
Define student responsibility in daily labs, Bistro service, cleanup, and advanced work.

## Leadership Principle
Leadership should emerge through competence. Culinary 2 should feel like an honor and a higher-responsibility space, but not an elitist club and not a generic leadership camp.

## Current Bistro Teams
Student leadership is organized around the five current Bistro teams: **Service, Barista, Line Cooks, Support, and Expo / Floaters**. See `02_Bistro/SOPs/` for team-specific opening/service/closing duties. "FOH/BOH," "Management Staff," and "Service Staff" are retired team labels and should not appear in current role documentation.

## Role Framework
| Role | Team | Duties |
|---|---|---|
| Station lead | Line Cooks | Checks mise en place, assigns station tasks, watches cleanliness, reports blockers |
| Bistro manager | Cross-team | Tracks overall prep/service status and communicates with instructor |
| Expo / Floaters | Expo / Floaters | Owns and calls production tickets from Expo, tracks cues and whole-order status, verifies quality and completeness, coordinates Line Cooks-to-Service handoff, and deploys trained support while keeping Expo covered |
| Inventory assistant | Cross-team | Notes shortages, labels, substitutions, and storage concerns |
| Sanitation lead | Support | Monitors sanitizer, dish flow, trash, towels, closing checklist |
| Service lead | Service | Supports host/check-in, POS and queue flow, attentive guest service, LATTE recovery, and room reset |
| Barista lead | Barista | Runs opening/closing checklist, drink quality, and station stocking |
| Steward/dish lead | Support | Runs dish system, stocks clean wares, protects flow |
| Peer mentor | Cross-team | Helps newer or weaker students without doing the work for them |

## Authority Boundaries
Student leaders may coordinate, remind, report, and model. They may not shame peers, approve unsafe equipment use, change grades, override instructor directions, or handle conflicts beyond their role.

During active production ticket flow, one trained Floater remains on Expo. Other trained Floaters deploy through **Expo → Line Cooks → Service → Barista → Support → Expo** using **Scan → Identify → Communicate → Act → Reassess** and the current `../SOPs/Expo_Floaters_Team_SOP.md`.

## Leadership Evidence
- Arrives prepared.
- Sees work without being told.
- Communicates calmly.
- Helps the station recover.
- Protects safety and sanitation.
- Leaves clear notes.
- Makes others better without taking over.


## Current Five-Team Training and Rotation

Use Service, Barista, Line Cooks, Support, and Expo / Floaters. The same instructional teams carry into Bistro and remain in each position for two consecutive services. Service 1 develops familiarity; Service 2 develops independence. Culinary 1 and Culinary 2 share one repeating ten-service cycle.

Life of an Order remains the foundation. Train Life of an Order; Service + Barista; Line Cooks + Support; Expo / Floaters + mock service; additional mock/check/reflection; targeted final training; real Bistro Prep; and Bistro Service. Thursday prep retains weekly lab jobs; Friday follows Bistro-position duties.
