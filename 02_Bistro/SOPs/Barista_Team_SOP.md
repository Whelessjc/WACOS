# Barista Team SOP

Current as of August 26, 2026

## Mission

**Barista owns beverage and pastry production and service.** Current recipes, drink builds, inventories, pars, and specials remain in current stock, recipe, menu, or prep references.

## Opening

- Enter Kitchen Mode; clean and sanitize.
- Review current menu, recipes, stock, pars, specials, and known orders.
- Set up authorized equipment and supplies.
- Establish the active-ticket holder and completed-ticket stabber.
- Report missing stock or equipment problems; do not invent workarounds.

## During Service

- Keep active tickets on the holder during production.
- Read tickets fully and prepare to current recipes and standards.
- Work safely, clean as you go, and coordinate timing and handoff with Tournant / Expo and Dining.
- Move released completed tickets to the stabber.
- Communicate delays and shortages.

## Closing

- Complete tickets; clean, sanitize, store, label, restock, and reset.
- Shut down authorized equipment correctly and report problems.
- Earlier classes reset for the next class; Culinary 2 completes final shutdown.
