# Barista Team SOP

Current as of October 25, 2026.

## Mission

**Barista owns beverage and pastry production and service.** Current recipes, drink builds, inventories, pars, and specials remain in current stock, recipe, menu, and prep references.

Students do not need to memorize every drink before service. They need to know how to operate Barista accurately using current build references.

**Barista operating rhythm:**

**Open → Organize → Receive Ticket → Use Build Reference → Produce → Verify → Handoff → Complete Ticket → Clean/Restock → Reset/Close**

## Opening

- Arrive On Time and Work Ready; move with graceful urgency.
- Clean and sanitize the station.
- Review the current menu, build references, stock, pars, specials, and known orders.
- Set up authorized equipment and supplies.
- Confirm the ticket printer and small table between POS and Barista are ready.
- Establish the ticket-control area, handoff table, and completed-ticket stabber. When dual rails are used, **FUTURE** means not yet released and **CURRENT** means actively owed.
- Report missing stock or equipment problems; do not invent workarounds.

## Thursday Setup and Friday Verification

Use the current menu and build references rather than inventing pars or drink formulas. Give each setup task an owner and visible completion status.

- [ ] Current drink/pastry menu, build references, known changes, shortages, and assigned production are available.
- [ ] Verify ingredients, cups/lids, serving tools, cleaning supplies, and other menu-specific stock; prepare/store/label under current procedures.
- [ ] Identify equipment and supplies at their working locations; identify any setup that must wait until Friday.
- [ ] Prepare ticket receiving/control, active work, and handoff areas so orders remain visible and separate from clutter.
- [ ] Assign who receives/calls tickets, makes items, verifies/hands off, and restocks/resets. Students may combine duties when practical; these are station tasks, not new Bistro jobs.
- [ ] Have students locate and explain the current builds using references; rehearse the ticket-to-handoff sequence before live service when readiness requires it. Memorization is not required.
- [ ] Record remaining Friday activation work, shortages, owner, and handoff. Setup does not mean leaving food out or equipment operating overnight.

On Friday, verify actual stock, authorized equipment readiness, build access, role clarity, and ticket/handoff flow before production. Report unresolved gaps to the instructor. During service, communicate delays or loss of station control early and use the current reset/clean/restock routine.

## Ticket and Production Flow

1. POS enters the order; the Barista ticket prints on the small table between POS and Barista.
2. Whole-order tickets are organized **Food → Beverage → Pastry**. Barista/Pastry sees the whole order but produces only its assigned beverage/pastry items: **See the whole order. Own your part.**
3. If the order is FUTURE, hold it without producing until release. When it becomes CURRENT, Barista retrieves/releases the ticket and calls **“Order in: [items].”**
4. The Barista team acknowledges with callbacks.
5. Keep the CURRENT ticket controlled in the active-ticket area.
6. Prepare the complete Barista/Pastry portion using current build references/recipes and confirmed modifications.
7. Verify the order before handoff.
8. Group completed Barista/Pastry items together and place the ticket with them on the handoff table.
9. Call the order ready. Service takes and delivers it.
10. Move the ticket to the stabber only after the order leaves Barista.

Barista does not wait on Line Cooks except for true delivery orders. Dine-in and takeout guests may receive beverages or pastries while hot food is still working.

## During Service

- Keep active tickets controlled on the holder and the handoff table organized.
- Work safely and clean as you go.
- Use current build references rather than guessing or relying on memory when uncertain.
- Communicate **getting low, backup, 86,** delays, modifications, and completed orders early and clearly.
- Coordinate handoff with Service and operational status with Expo / Floaters.
- Stay aware of the whole operation without abandoning Barista responsibilities. Ask: **What do you notice? What does it mean? What needs to happen next?**
- Restock and reset continuously when service conditions allow.

## Reset and Closing

- Complete active tickets before closing the production flow.
- Clean, sanitize, store, label, date mark, restock, and reset under current procedures.
- Shut down authorized equipment correctly and report problems.
- Clear the holder, handoff, stabber, and ticket-printer area.
- Earlier classes reset for the next class; the final responsible class completes final shutdown.
