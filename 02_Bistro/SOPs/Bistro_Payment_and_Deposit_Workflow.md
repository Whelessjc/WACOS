# Bistro Payment and Deposit Workflow

Current as of September 13, 2026

## Purpose

Document the instructor workflow for recording Bistro orders and payments, producing kitchen tickets, reconciling service records, and assembling the deposit packet. This SOP records current program practice and does not replace district or bookkeeper requirements.

## Governing Order-Control Rule

**Requests can come from many places. Production orders come from Square.**

Phone calls, email, Google Forms, early walk-ins, lunch requests, and other instructor-accepted channels may begin an order request. Once an order is accepted and released for production, it must enter the normal Square workflow so Barista and Line Cooks receive production through the same controlled ticket system.

A classic diner ticket may be used as a temporary intake tool when a request is taken away from POS or by phone. It is not a kitchen production ticket. Once the request has been correctly entered and saved in Square, the handwritten intake ticket must not be sent to Barista or Line Cooks as a parallel production order.

## Accepted Payment and Redemption Types

| Type | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Cash | Accepted monetary payment; record in Square as cash. |
| Check | Accepted monetary payment; checks must be payable to **WAHS** and recorded in Square as check. |
| Approved PBIS incentive | Noncash meal redemption; record as a comp using the `PBIS Incentive` reason. |
| Bistro coin | Noncash meal redemption for one meal; record as a comp using the `Bistro Coin` reason. |

The Bistro does not accept card payments under the current instructor-confirmed workflow. Square is the sales-record and kitchen-ticket system; Square does not process the cash or checks.

## Remote and Advance Orders

### Two States

Keep future requests conceptually separate from released production orders:

1. **REQUEST RECEIVED** — the Bistro has the request, but it is not yet time to send it into production.
2. **ORDER RELEASED** — the request is entered in Square, named, and saved. Under the current Bistro Square configuration, saving sends the appropriate production tickets to connected printers. The saved check remains open for later payment.

Do not save a future order hours early merely to store it in Square if saving will immediately print production tickets. Hold the request until the appropriate production-release time.

### Request Intake

An order request may arrive through:

- Google Form;
- email;
- phone;
- an in-person request before school;
- a walk-in request at another appropriate time;
- another instructor-accepted channel.

The person receiving the request records enough information to enter it accurately in Square, including the guest/customer name, requested items/modifications, and needed pickup/service timing when applicable. A classic diner ticket may be used for this temporary intake.

Do not promise an order the Bistro cannot safely or reasonably fulfill. Instructor judgment governs acceptance, timing, capacity, menu availability, allergens/food-safety concerns, and unusual requests.

### Release to Production in Square

When it is time for the accepted order to enter production:

1. Open a new check in Square.
2. Enter the actual menu items and confirmed modifications.
3. Go to **Actions → Edit Name/Notes** and give the check the guest/customer name so it can be identified later.
4. Verify the order before release.
5. Select **Save**, not Pay.
6. Saving the check releases the order through the configured Square production-ticket printers while leaving the check open for later payment.
7. The handwritten/intake request is now only supporting intake evidence; production follows the Square tickets.

**Do not send the diner ticket to Barista or Line Cooks.**

### Retrieve and Pay Later

When the guest/customer is ready to pay:

1. Select **View Checks** from the main Square screen.
2. Navigate to the open/custom checks view as configured on the Bistro POS.
3. Locate the named check.
4. Reopen it and verify the order.
5. Add or correct items only through the normal Square workflow if needed.
6. Select **Pay** and complete the current approved cash/check/redemption process.
7. Close the check only after the transaction is correctly settled.

The operational distinction is:

**SAVE = release the accepted order to production and keep the check open.**

**PAY = settle and close the check when payment is received.**

## PBIS and Bistro Coin Setup

Bistro coins are wooden tokens with the West Ashley Culinary logo. They are issued through classroom-management PBIS and may be redeemed for one Bistro meal. Other approved PBIS incentives may also cover a meal.

In Square:

1. Create separate comp reasons named `PBIS Incentive` and `Bistro Coin`.
2. Ring the actual meal items so the kitchen receives an accurate ticket.
3. Send the order to the kitchen through the normal service flow.
4. Comp the item or entire ticket using the correct reason.
5. Close the ticket at a $0 balance.

Do not record these redemptions as `Other payment`, gift card, cash, or check. They are comps, not collected revenue.

Square includes comped items in gross item activity because gross sales are calculated before discounts and comps. Use net sales and payment-method totals for deposit reconciliation, and use the separate comp report to review PBIS and Bistro coin activity.

## During Service

- Enter every released order in Square before or as it is sent to production.
- Do not operate handwritten, email, Google Form, or verbal requests as a parallel kitchen-ticket system.
- For an unpaid accepted order, use the named open-check workflow above and collect payment later.
- Select the payment type actually received: cash or check.
- Confirm checks are payable to WAHS.
- Use the correct comp reason for PBIS incentives and Bistro coins.
- Keep cash, checks, and redemption tokens controlled during service.
- Correct entry errors using the approved Square correction method; do not disguise errors as PBIS or Bistro coin comps.

## If Square Is Unavailable

1. Use sequentially numbered backup receipts.
2. Record the order, amount, payment type, and receipt number.
3. Communicate the order directly to Line Cooks and/or Barista.
4. When Square returns, the instructor or Teaching Assistant re-enters every sale individually.
5. Enter the actual items, correct tender, and backup receipt number; do not enter a lump total.
6. Prevent duplicate production by marking and communicating that the re-entry is a completed outage transaction.
7. Reconcile the re-entry total to backup receipts and collected payments.
8. Retain backup receipts with the deposit documentation.

The Square-outage process is the exception to the normal rule that production orders come from Square.

## Starting Till

The approved starting till is **$180**:

| Denomination | Quantity | Amount |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| $20 | 3 | $60 |
| $10 | 5 | $50 |
| $5 | 10 | $50 |
| $1 | 20 | $20 |
| **Total** |  | **$180** |

## Till Variance

For every variance, regardless of amount:

1. Recount the drawer.
2. Confirm the $180 starting till.
3. Compare cash and checks to Square.
4. Review comps, voids, refunds, open checks, and backup receipts.
5. Document any remaining variance.
6. Notify the bookkeeper.

No unexplained variance is acceptable. A variance alone must not be used to charge, accuse, discipline, or grade a student.

## Service Close

1. Review open Square checks and resolve each one appropriately; do not accidentally leave paid/picked-up orders open.
2. Confirm cash, check, PBIS, and Bistro coin entries use the correct type.
3. Count and secure cash and checks.
4. Confirm checks are payable to WAHS.
5. Download the detailed Square sales transactions report.
6. Review net sales and payment-method totals against cash and checks received.
7. Review the comp report for PBIS Incentive and Bistro Coin usage.
8. Reconcile any backup receipts used during an outage.
9. Complete the required bookkeeper and deposit forms.
10. Attach the detailed Square transactions report and required supporting records to the deposit.

## Deposit Timing

- Complete the deposit on Friday when time permits.
- If Friday completion is not practical, secure the funds and complete the deposit Monday.
- Follow current district and bookkeeper requirements for custody, signatures, storage, delivery, and record retention.
- The instructor or Teaching Assistant may independently reconcile cash and checks, assemble the deposit materials, and deliver them to the bookkeeper. Students do not perform final reconciliation or deposit delivery.
- Refunds, guest comps, and financial adjustments require instructor approval. Students may complete ordinary LATTE service recovery within their assigned authority.

## Control Boundaries

District and bookkeeper requirements govern custody, signatures, storage, delivery, and record retention. The instructor and Teaching Assistant handle cash; either may complete reconciliation and deliver the deposit to the bookkeeper.
