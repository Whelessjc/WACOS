# Service Team SOP

Current as of September 12, 2026.

## Mission

**Service owns the guest experience.** Service responsibilities include host/check-in, POS, runner, busser, dining-room readiness, guest communication, delivery, clearing, and reset. Expo belongs to Expo / Floaters.

Service is not standing at the host stand waiting for something obvious to happen. Service circulates with purpose, sees the room, and takes care of guests throughout the Life of an Order.

## Opening — Graceful Urgency

Guests may already be present when students arrive. Move through Work Ready and opening with **graceful urgency**: purposeful movement, calm communication, immediate action, no running/panic.

- Prepare and clean the host stand, POS area, dining room, self-seating tables, available printed menus, reservations, specials, service supplies, handoff routes, and serviceware.
- Confirm the Promethean/current primary menu display is ready when used; printed menus may be offered as a Service resource when useful rather than automatically tied to queue numbers.
- Confirm Square and receipt supplies under instructor/Teaching Assistant control.
- Stage OnTime check-in, queue numbers, sign-out, and hall passes.
- Review dietary-alert escalation, LATTE, current unavailable items, and Expo / Floaters and Barista handoffs.
- Acknowledge waiting guests even when the operation is still opening. Do not leave a guest wondering whether anyone sees them.

## Host and ILT Check-In

1. Greet every student first: **“Good morning.”**
2. Do not judge whether the student belongs before greeting.
3. Ask whether the student is joining the Bistro for ILT.
4. If yes, have the student scan into OnTime.
5. If OnTime approves, ask whether the student is dining or just hanging out.
6. Give an individual queue number to a guest who is dining or ordering. Give no number to a non-ordering guest.
7. If OnTime denies entry, apologize and wish the student a good day.

Students leaving the ILT space sign out and take a hall pass. Guests choose any available seat; Service does not assign or escort guests to tables.

If OnTime/Kiosk mode fails, use only the current instructor-approved fallback. Do not invent attendance approval or treat an improvised paper list as permanent policy.

## POS Order-Taking

- The Square POS host/cashier calls the queue number, makes eye contact, smiles, and greets the guest.
- Take and confirm the order, repeat it back, record modifications accurately, and flag dietary alerts without guessing.
- Enter the order in Square through the current transaction workflow.
- Service students do **not** take seated-table orders.

## During Service — See the Room

- Receive completed food orders from Expo / Floaters and completed Barista orders from the Barista handoff; deliver them accurately and promptly.
- Circulate with purpose rather than clustering/hovering at the stand.
- Continuously scan for low or empty beverages, finished plates, missing flatware, condiments or napkins, food or trash on the floor, guests looking for help, guest body language, waiting food or drinks, and queue numbers ready to be returned.
- Recognize where guests are in the Life of an Order: arriving, ordering, waiting, eating, needing something, finished, leaving, or requiring table reset.
- Use: **What do you notice? What does it mean? What needs to happen next?**
- Whenever delivering a drink, pastry, food, flatware, condiment, or other item, ask whether the guest needs anything else or is still waiting on food or Barista items.
- Leave the queue number while anything remains outstanding; retrieve it promptly when the order is complete. Queue numbers are not transferable.
- Clear and reset tables promptly without rushing guests.

A useful Service loop is:

**SCAN → APPROACH → ACT → MOVE → SCAN AGAIN**

## Guest Recovery

- Protect the system, assume good faith, and do not argue.
- For a questionable queue number or another concern, say **“Let me check on that for you.”**
- Use LATTE: **Listen, Acknowledge, Take action, Thank, Ensure satisfaction.**
- Correct ordinary service problems within assigned authority. Escalate refunds, comps, financial adjustments, allergens, safety concerns, repeated remakes, escalating behavior, and unresolved complaints.

## Reset and Closing

- Clear and reset guest areas, host/POS stations, service supplies, handoff routes, floors, and trash.
- Return queue numbers, hall passes, and service tools to assigned locations.
- Restock approved supplies and report shortages or failures.
- Earlier classes reset for the next class; the final responsible class completes final shutdown.

Service does not perform final financial reconciliation or deposit delivery. Those controls remain with the instructor and Teaching Assistant.
