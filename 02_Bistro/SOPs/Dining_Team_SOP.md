# Dining Team SOP

Current as of August 26, 2026

## Mission

**Dining owns the guest experience.** Responsibilities include host, server, runner, busser, dining-room readiness, guest communication, table service, clearing, and reset. Expo belongs to Tournant / Expo.

## Opening

- Prepare the host stand, server station, dining room, tables, menus, reservations, specials, service supplies, and clean serviceware.
- Confirm Square and receipt supplies under instructor/Teaching Assistant control.
- Review guest communication, dietary-alert escalation, LATTE, and Tournant / Expo handoff.

## During Service

- Greet guests promptly and support the current self-seating system. Guests choose any available spot; Dining does not assign tables or escort guests to seats.
- Communicate clearly and enter orders accurately.
- Follow current beverage, delivery, check-back, clearing, and reset standards.
- Receive completed orders from Tournant / Expo and deliver them correctly.
- Use LATTE: Listen, Acknowledge, Take action, Thank, and Ensure satisfaction.
- Escalate refunds, comps, financial adjustments, allergens, safety concerns, repeated remakes, escalating behavior, and unresolved complaints.

## Closing

- Clear and reset guest areas, stations, supplies, floors, and trash.
- Report shortages or failures.
- Earlier classes reset for the next class; Culinary 2 completes final shutdown.

Dining does not perform final financial reconciliation or deposit delivery. Those controls remain with the instructor and Teaching Assistant.
