## Current Teams and Rotation Rhythm

The five current teams are **Dining, Barista, Kitchen, Support, and Tournant / Expo**. Expo belongs to Tournant / Expo, not Dining. The same instructional teams carry into service and normally remain in each position for two consecutive services in one shared ten-service cycle.

Thursday prep retains weekly lab cleaning jobs. Friday service follows Bistro-position Opening, During Service, and Closing responsibilities. Earlier classes reset for the next class; Culinary 2 completes final shutdown.

## Confirmation Boundary

The host/OnTime/ILT check-in, queue-number, POS order-taking, production ticket ownership, handoff, LATTE, team cleaning, Support shutdown, and Tournant / Expo flows are instructor-confirmed. Current payment and deposit controls remain governed by `Bistro_Payment_and_Deposit_Workflow.md` and instructor/Teaching Assistant authority.
