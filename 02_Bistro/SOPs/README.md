# Bistro SOPs

Current as of August 26, 2026

Current durable SOPs cover Life of an Order, Service Standards, Dining, Barista, Kitchen, Support, Tournant / Expo, and Payment/Deposit Workflow.

The five current teams are **Dining, Barista, Kitchen, Support, and Tournant / Expo**. Expo belongs to Tournant / Expo, not Dining. Teams remain in each position for two consecutive services.

Durable team SOPs use **Opening**, **During Service**, and **Closing**. Variable menus, pars, specials, inventories, builds, ingredients, and menu-specific mise en place remain in current prep, stock, menu, recipe, and production references. Management Staff and Service Staff are retired team names; FOH/BOH are industry vocabulary, not the current structure.
