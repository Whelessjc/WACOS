# Support Team SOP

Current as of September 12, 2026.

## Mission and Boundary

**Support owns stewarding, dish flow, and operational cleanliness. Support is not everyone else's cleaning crew.** Every station cleans and resets/closes its own station; Support keeps shared systems moving and progressively closes shared operational areas.

Support operates inside the permanent Bistro Rhythm. Students arrive Work Ready, open the shared systems, own their area, communicate, stay aware, remain useful, clean continuously, reset, and close when required.

## Opening — Graceful Urgency

- Arrive On Time and Work Ready; move with graceful urgency.
- Enter practical kitchen work only when Kitchen Ready and authorized.
- Set up wash, rinse, and sanitize systems correctly and verify sanitizer with the required test strip.
- Set up and verify the dish machine and utensil presoak according to current procedures.
- Establish scrape/presort, wash, rinse, sanitize, air-dry, and clean-return areas.
- Prepare racks, towels, laundry, trash, floor tools, carts, and required hazard signs.
- Report broken or failing equipment; do not create workarounds.

## During Service — Protect the Shared Systems

- Maintain continuous scrape, presort, wash, rinse, sanitize, air-dry, and return flow.
- Scan for shared needs: dirty dishes, full trash, linen flow, spills, blocked paths, depleted wares, and sanitation concerns.
- Maintain sanitizer, shared counters and storage, floors, laundry, and trash.
- Return clean wares and communicate shortages early.
- Require each station to move its dirty equipment and dishes into dish flow and clean its own work area.
- Request Expo / Floaters help before a bottleneck develops.
- Stay aware of the whole operation without abandoning Support responsibilities. Ask: **What do you notice? What does it mean? What needs to happen next?**
- During lulls, be useful by restocking, returning clean wares, clearing shared bottlenecks, and progressively resetting areas rather than waiting for an adult to assign work.

## Progressive Shutdown

The goal is to close the back of the kitchen progressively and move the active operation forward. Shutdown may occur in phases throughout the day.

1. Begin with a cart and sweep from the back of the kitchen forward, moving items that do not belong.
2. Verify food is properly stored, labeled, and date marked under current procedures.
3. Move dirty equipment and dishes forward into dish flow.
4. Clean from top to bottom in this order:
   1. food and storage
   2. equipment and dishes
   3. counters and surfaces
   4. lower surfaces and shelves
   5. floors
5. Sweep and mop floors last.
6. When an area is fully closed, turn off its lights and continue moving forward.

The end state is to have only the triple sink forward, line, and dish machine active until final production and dish flow are complete.

## Reset and Closing

- Confirm all dishes are washed, rinsed, sanitized, air-dried, and returned correctly.
- Empty and clean sinks; complete the dish-machine close when final shutdown is required; store racks and tools.
- Complete shared counters, storage support, floors, laundry, trash, and reset.
- Report shortages, damage, incomplete station closes, or failures instead of silently absorbing another station's work.
- Earlier classes **reset** for the next class; the final responsible class **closes** under current procedures.

**Clean as You Go** maintains shared systems while operating. **Reset** leaves them ready for the next group. **Close** performs final shutdown when required.
