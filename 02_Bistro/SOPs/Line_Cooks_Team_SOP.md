# Line Cooks Team SOP

Current as of September 12, 2026.

## Mission

**Line Cooks own food production.** Menu-specific ingredients, equipment, pars, and mise en place remain on current recipes, prep lists, and production materials.

Line Cooks operate inside the permanent Bistro Rhythm. They arrive Work Ready, open the station, own the area, communicate, stay aware, remain useful, execute the work, clean as they go, reset, and close when required.

## Opening — Graceful Urgency

- Arrive On Time and Work Ready; move with graceful urgency.
- Enter practical kitchen work only when Kitchen Ready and authorized.
- Review current recipes/build references, prep lists, pars, expectations, and known orders.
- Complete mise en place with authorized equipment.
- Verify labels, storage, holding, thermometers, sanitation, and quality controls.
- Prepare visible physical production cues appropriate to the current menu, such as plates, pans, vessels, or key food components.
- Communicate shortages, safety concerns, and delays; confirm the Expo / Floaters handoff.

## Production Ticket Ownership

**Line Cooks do not see or read production tickets. Expo / Floaters owns the tickets.**

1. Expo reads each new production ticket and calls **“Order in”** with the required items.
2. Line Cooks call back with **“Heard”** so Expo knows the call was received.
3. Line Cooks immediately place a physical production cue representing each called dish.
4. Line Cooks work from Expo's calls, callbacks, physical cues, sequencing, and status communication.
5. Expo watches the cooks and cues, tracks the whole order, verifies completeness and quality, and releases it to Service.

Do not create an untracked verbal promise or remove a production cue before the item is accounted for.

## Service Communication

- **Order in:** Expo is calling a new production ticket into production.
- **Callback / Heard:** confirm that the call was received and understood.
- **Fire the ___:** begin the named item now.
- **All day:** the total currently needed across all active tickets.
- **On the fly:** an urgent priority needed as quickly and safely as possible.
- **86:** an item is unavailable.
- **Getting low:** inventory is approaching a critical level.
- **Backup:** replenishment or operational help is needed or moving forward.
- **In the weeds:** a station is falling behind and needs immediate communication or support.
- **Lowboy:** under-counter refrigerated storage at the line.

Communicate delays, shortages, re-fires, quality problems, and needed help early. Never hide a problem until the order fails.

## During Service — Own the Area and See the Operation

- Follow current recipes/build references and approved methods.
- Control time, temperature, quality, doneness, holding, and sensory standards.
- Clean as you go, plate accurately, watch physical cues, and sequence work from Expo's calls.
- Give explicit status updates; do not assume Expo can infer readiness or delays.
- Release completed food through Expo / Floaters for whole-order verification and Line Cooks-to-Service handoff.
- Stay aware of the whole operation without abandoning Line Cooks responsibilities. Ask: **What do you notice? What does it mean? What needs to happen next?**
- Be useful during lulls: restock, reset, communicate, prepare authorized backups, or address visible station needs rather than waiting for an adult to create work.
- Report allergen, safety, equipment, holding, cooling, storage, discard, or release concerns immediately.

## Reset and Closing

- Confirm with Expo that all active orders and physical production cues are closed.
- Complete approved storage, labeling, date marking, cooling, cleaning, shutdown, and reset.
- Move dirty equipment and dishes into dish flow; every station cleans its own station.
- Restock and leave the station ready for whoever comes next.
- Report shortages, waste, damage, incomplete work, and failures.
- Earlier classes **reset** for the next class; the final responsible class **closes** under current procedures.

**Clean as You Go** maintains the station while operating. **Reset** leaves it ready for the next group. **Close** performs final shutdown when required.

Instructor authority over equipment, food safety, allergens, food release, holding, cooling, storage, and discard is unchanged.
