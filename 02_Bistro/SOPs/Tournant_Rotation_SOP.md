# Tournant / Expo Team SOP

Current as of August 26, 2026

## Mission

**Tournant / Expo owns the flow of the operation.** Expo is the team’s home base. During active service one Tournant student remains responsible for Expo/pass while other trained and authorized members deploy.

## Opening

- Clean, sanitize, organize, and stock Expo/pass.
- Prepare the active kitchen ticket holder and completed-ticket stabber.
- Confirm Kitchen-to-Dining handoff.
- Review absences, gaps, deliveries, lunch-opening needs, and bottlenecks.
- Assign the Expo anchor.

## During Service — Expo

- Keep active tickets on the holder until completed orders leave the kitchen.
- Compare completed orders with tickets.
- Maintain the pass and coordinate Kitchen-to-Dining handoff.
- Communicate missing/delayed items and protect order flow.
- Move released completed tickets to the stabber.
- Maintain Expo readiness and reset.

## During Service — Floating Priorities

1. Cover an absence or uncovered position.
2. Respond to immediate operational needs.
3. Delivery, running, or lunch-opening support.
4. Help a station falling behind.
5. Retrieve or restock supplies.
6. Complete standing useful work.
7. Cross-train for upcoming positions.
8. Continue scanning for useful work.

**Tournant does not mean standby or free time.**

Use **Expo → Kitchen → Dining → Barista → Support → Expo** and ask: **What do you notice? What does it mean? What needs to happen next?**

## Closing

- Clear and reset Expo/pass, holder, stabber, and handoff area.
- Confirm deployed members finish closing at their last assignment.
- Remove bottlenecks, restock, and report unfinished work or problems.
- Earlier classes reset for the next class; Culinary 2 completes final shutdown.
