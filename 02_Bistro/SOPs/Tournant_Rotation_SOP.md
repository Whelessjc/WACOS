# Tournant Rotation SOP

Current as of August 26, 2026

## Purpose

The Tournant is the Bistro's fifth rotation and provides flexible coverage where the operation needs it most. It is not a fixed station and does not create independent student management authority.

## Assignment Priority

1. Fill an absence.
2. Respond to the most immediate operational need.
3. Complete delivery, running, or lunch-opening work assigned by the instructor.
4. Help a rotation that is falling behind.
5. Restock and reset.
6. Cross-train.
7. Find other useful work.

**Standby does not mean standing around.**

## Opening

- Report ready and receive the first assignment.
- Check known absences and coverage gaps.
- Confirm the location, task, standard, and person to report to.
- Prepare assigned delivery, running, or lunch-opening materials.

## During Service

- Work the assigned coverage role to that rotation's standard.
- Watch for bottlenecks while remaining accountable for the current assignment.
- Ask before abandoning or changing an assigned task.
- Move to the next priority when redirected.
- Communicate completed work and unresolved needs.

## Closing and Reset

- Finish the closing duties of the last assigned rotation.
- Restock and reset shared supplies and equipment.
- Report incomplete work or shortages.
- Earlier classes reset for the next class.
- Culinary 2, during the double path of Paths 5 and 8, supports final closing and shutdown before Planning in Paths 6 and 7.

## Assessment

The instructor or Teaching Assistant records Tournant evidence on the universal Bistro Observation Record using the same Opening/Readiness, Active Service, and Closing/Reset checkpoints and E/M/C/N/NO codes used for every rotation.
