# Tournant / Expo Team SOP

Current as of August 26, 2026

## Purpose

Tournant / Expo is the Bistro's fifth rotation and **owns the flow of the operation**. Expo is the team's home base and anchor responsibility. One Tournant student keeps Expo covered during active Kitchen ticket flow while other Tournant students provide flexible coverage where the operation needs it most.

Tournant / Expo does not create independent student management authority. Students assist only within existing training, authorization, safety, and instructor-control boundaries.

## Operating Loop

**Expo -> Kitchen -> Dining -> Barista -> Support -> Expo**

Ask:

- What do you notice?
- What does it mean?
- What needs to happen next?

**Standby does not mean standing around.**

## Opening

- Clean and organize the Expo/pass area between Kitchen and Dining.
- Set up the active Kitchen ticket holder and completed-ticket stabber; stage pens/markers and current menu/order references.
- Confirm the handoff/pass is clear, sanitary, and ready for completed plates/orders.
- Review the current menu/specials and expected Kitchen -> Expo -> Dining flow.
- Know the five team assignments for the shift and identify absences or uncovered positions with the instructor.
- Assign one Tournant student as the Expo anchor; Expo must remain covered while active Kitchen tickets or completed food are moving.
- Identify likely floating needs for the shift: absence coverage, running/delivery, lunch-opening support, restocking, or other instructor-directed work.
- Do a whole-operation readiness scan: Kitchen, Dining, Barista, Support, then return to Expo.

## During Service

### Expo Anchor

- Keep each active Kitchen ticket on the ticket holder until the complete order leaves the Kitchen; then place the completed ticket on the ticket stabber.
- Compare the completed order to the ticket before release: correct items/modifications, complete order, and presentation/quality readiness.
- Escalate allergen, food-safety, or other instructor-controlled concerns rather than guessing.
- Coordinate Kitchen -> Dining handoff and communicate missing items, delays, or unclear tickets immediately.
- Keep the pass organized; do not allow completed food, active tickets, dirty dishes, or random supplies to pile up.
- Keep Expo covered whenever active Kitchen tickets or completed food are moving.

### Floating Tournant Work

Priority order:

1. Cover an absence or instructor-assigned gap.
2. Respond to an immediate operational need.
3. Complete delivery, running, or lunch-opening work assigned by the instructor.
4. Help a station that is falling behind within existing training/authorization.
5. Retrieve/restock needed supplies when that protects service flow.
6. Complete standing useful work and reset shared areas.
7. Cross-train for upcoming positions when the operation is under control.
8. Continue scanning for useful work.

- Communicate completed work and unresolved needs.
- Return to Expo/whole-operation awareness after completing deployed work.
- Do not abandon an assigned coverage role without communicating.

## Closing / Reset

- Confirm all active Kitchen tickets are completed/accounted for and the ticket holder is clear.
- Clean/sanitize and reset Expo/pass, ticket holder/stabber area, and shared handoff surfaces.
- Return borrowed pens, markers, menus, trays, hot plates, containers, and other shared equipment to their homes.
- Check with Dining, Kitchen, Barista, and Support for unresolved service/closing needs.
- Help the operation finish remaining closing bottlenecks only after Expo responsibilities are secured.
- Report shortages, broken equipment, missed orders, or recurring flow problems to the instructor.
- Complete a final whole-operation scan; no abandoned product, dishes, tickets, or unfinished shared work.
- Earlier classes reset for the next class.
- Culinary 2, during the double path of Paths 5 and 8, supports final closing and shutdown before Planning in Paths 6 and 7.
- Leave Expo ready for the next service.

## Assessment

The instructor or Teaching Assistant records Tournant / Expo evidence on the universal Bistro Observation Record using the same Opening/Readiness, Active Service, and Closing/Reset checkpoints and E/M/C/N/NO codes used for every rotation.

Evidence should include both Expo execution and the student's ability to recognize and respond usefully to operational needs without abandoning assigned responsibilities.
