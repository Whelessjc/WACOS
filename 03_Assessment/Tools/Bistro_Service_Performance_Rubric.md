# Bistro Service Performance Rubric and Grading Calculator

Adopted November 10, 2026. Instructor and Teaching Assistant reference; instructor review before student distribution.

Record one overall performance score from 0–5 and a separate self-reflection out of 10. Consider the complete work cycle: readiness, service, productive downtime, safe and sanitary work, communication, teamwork, and complete reset. Opening readiness remains an operating gate; reset has no separate score column.

## Passing Performance

The student completes a meaningful share of assigned work, responds to coaching, and contributes to a complete reset, with appropriate support.

A student may struggle, make mistakes, or need substantial help and still meet this threshold. Assess what the student demonstrates with approved supports. Asking necessary safety or authorization questions is appropriate performance.

## Failing Performance Boundary

**After clear directions, appropriate support, and an opportunity to improve, the student repeatedly disengages and leaves significant assigned responsibilities for others to complete.**

Needing help alone does not define failure. Record the unfinished responsibilities and response to coaching, rather than inferring attitude or motivation. Levels 0–2 describe performance below the minimum contribution standard. Absence and insufficient observation are not earned level-zero scores.

## Overall Performance

| Level | Description | Observable evidence | Conversion |
|---:|---|---|---:|
| 5 | Exceptional ownership | Meets the full standard, anticipates needs, and strengthens the operation while completing assigned work and reset. Leadership can be quiet. | 100% |
| 4 | Dependable performance | Completes assigned work and reset to standard. Uses references, communicates, and responds to occasional coaching. | 90% |
| 3 | Meaningful contribution | Completes a meaningful share of assigned work, responds to coaching, and contributes to complete reset. Skill or pace may be uneven; appropriate support may be substantial. | 75% |
| 2 | Unfinished responsibilities | After clear directions, appropriate support, and an opportunity to improve, repeatedly disengages and leaves significant assigned responsibilities for others to complete. | 50% |
| 1 | Minimal contribution | Demonstrates very little useful assigned work despite clear directions, appropriate support, and opportunity. Others complete nearly all responsibilities. | 25% |
| 0 | No demonstrated performance | No assessable performance demonstrated despite an available opportunity. Instructor reviews the circumstances. | 0% |

A 4 allows normal learning and occasional coaching. A 5 does not require a leadership title, taking over another student’s work, or work outside training or authorization. A successful rush does not erase abandoned closing responsibilities.

## Self Reflection Worth 10 Points

| Maximum points | Evidence in the response |
|---:|---|
| 4 | Specific contribution, including reset. |
| 3 | Accurate explanation of what worked or needed improvement. |
| 3 | Concrete action for the next service. |

Award partial credit within each criterion. Grade evidence and thinking. An honest explanation of poor performance can earn full reflection credit; agreement with the adult score is not required. Reflection supports learning and does not replace observed performance.

## Grading Calculation

**Bistro grade out of 100 = (performance conversion × 0.90) + self-reflection points.**

Performance supplies up to 90 points and reflection up to 10. Retain the earned 0–5 performance level in the working record. Do not divide the raw level by five or combine the raw scores as points out of 15. The calculation applies to each observed Bistro service; aggregation across services remains an instructor implementation decision.

## Recording and Authority

Use brief notes for meaningful exceptions. For repeated disengagement, identify the assigned responsibility, directions/support provided, opportunity to improve, and work left for others. A student receiving substantial help can earn level 3 when the minimum meaningful contribution and reset are demonstrated.

Keep absence, unobserved work, and temporary missing-work placeholders distinct from earned performance levels. In particular, a gradebook missing-work placeholder of 1 is not an earned level 1 and must not be converted to 25%. Do not auto-fill blanks as four. Follow the [Assessment System](../06_Assessment_System.md) for unresolved absence, blank-entry, and adult-recording-failure procedures.

Instructor authority, approved accommodations, safety responses, and equipment authorization remain in force. This rubric assesses demonstrated Bistro work; discipline and release/return decisions remain separate. Do not automatically apply the same incident as a second Employability deduction. The Bistro conversion does not establish lab or Employability conversions.
