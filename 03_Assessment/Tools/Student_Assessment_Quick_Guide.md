# Student Assessment Quick Guide

> **Instructor review required before student use.**

# High School, High Standards

West Ashley Culinary grades what you can show through real work. Observation records help the instructor see patterns. A mark on a checklist is evidence; the instructor makes the final grading decision.

## Bistro

During Bistro service, an adult may record quick evidence during opening, active service, and closing. You are expected to:

- enter Kitchen Mode ready and understand your assignment;
- work safely, correctly, cleanly, and with clear communication;
- respond to coaching and changing service needs;
- protect the guest, food, production, or service standard for your role;
- complete shutdown and leave the area reset.

Codes are `E` exceptional evidence, `M` meets, `C` coaching needed, `N` not met, and `NO` not observed. `NO` is not failure. Bistro grades remain in Summative Assessments: Projects, Tests, and Bistro.

## Labs

Labs look at five areas: readiness and planning; safe, sanitary, organized work; technique and process; product result when appropriate; and shutdown, reset, and reflection.

The scale is `4` exceeds, `3` meets, `2` approaches, `1` does not yet meet, and `NO` insufficient evidence. The instructor may emphasize one or two focus criteria. A final product does not erase unsafe or weak process, and appearance does not outweigh the actual learning target. Labs remain in Formative Assessments and Labs.

## Employability

Employability is how you show you can be trusted in a kitchen. It includes readiness, punctuality, uniform and materials, communication, teamwork, initiative, coachability, station responsibility, productive use of time, cleanup/reset, and stewardship.

Students meeting normal expectations do not need a note every day. The instructor records meaningful coaching, repeated patterns, significant incidents, improvement, exceptional ownership, and leadership. One small mistake should not define the grading period. You will have a chance to review the evidence before the grade is finalized. Employability remains 20% of the quarter grade.

## What Applies Everywhere

- Safety, sanitation, and instructor directions are non-negotiable.
- Serious safety concerns receive immediate intervention and instructor review.
- Reflection supports direct performance evidence; it does not replace it.
- Your group result is not automatically your individual grade.
- You should receive timely feedback and a clear next step.
- Approved accommodations and supports are honored.


## Missing Bistro Work and Improvement

Bistro is normally Friday. If you will miss or have missed service, communicate with the instructor. For an excused absence, the gradebook initially shows 1 for missing work. You have five days to complete an approved choice-based makeup. The highest possible score is 90 on day 1, 80 on day 2, 70 on day 3, 60 on day 4, and 50 on day 5; after that the grade becomes 0. Possible makeups include ILT work, a morning shift, a restaurant review, making a dish at home, or another option approved by the instructor.

An unexcused no-call/no-show on a clearly communicated Bistro or major-project day earns 0 after the instructor verifies the official attendance classification and documented circumstances.

A missed Bistro service is not recreated. Later observation records and the next accumulated Bistro grade provide the opportunity to demonstrate improvement. A lab or applied skill may be reassessed case by case through an instructor-approved direct demonstration or focused practical.
