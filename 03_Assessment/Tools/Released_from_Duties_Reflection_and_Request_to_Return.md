## Required Learning

Complete this assignment independently using the existing recipe, prep list, station checklist, or SOP for the duties you were released from. Write your answers on notebook paper. Put your name, class, date, and assignment or station at the top. Identify the materials you used.

1. What were you responsible for completing?

2. Explain the complete work sequence, from preparation through cleanup and reset, in your own words.

3. What checks tell you the work is correct and complete?

4. What will you do when you need help, finish a task, or have a pause in work?

5. What specific action will demonstrate that you are ready to return?

Use specific details from your actual assignment. Explain the work in your own words. Copied directions, generic promises, and incomplete responses do not establish understanding. Correct missing or inaccurate work before requesting review. Use your approved learning supports.

## Request to Return

Submit the completed reflection and required learning, then request a readiness check. Be prepared to explain or demonstrate the relevant procedure. The instructor decides when review can occur without interrupting instruction or service and whether return is approved.

Return may occur the same day. You remain Released from Duties until the required work is complete, you request return, demonstrate readiness, and receive instructor approval. If requirements remain incomplete, release continues into subsequent classes. No fixed minimum time out is required.

While waiting for review or approval, continue existing assigned coursework. Do not resume practical duties without approval. Complete the Culinary Missed Lab / Make-Up Google Form when required for missed practical work; it does not replace this assignment.
