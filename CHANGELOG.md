# Changelog

## 2026-09-12 - Integrate Week 5 Bistro Launch Evidence and Opening-Curriculum Redesign

### Program Decisions

- Reframed Bistro as the authentic performance environment for fundamentals taught from Day 1 rather than a separate Week 4/5 unit: Weeks 1-3 build component habits, Week 4 assembles Prep/Service systems, and Week 5 remains the normal first-public-service target when readiness, safety, and calendar allow.
- Adopted the weekly arc **Monday-Wednesday — BUILD THE FUNDAMENTALS; Thursday — PLAN THE WORK; Friday — WORK THE PLAN**.
- Adopted permanent **Bistro Prep Rhythm** and **Bistro Rhythm** operating language, including Work Ready vs Kitchen Ready, graceful urgency, situational awareness across all five teams, and the teaching language **“I don't need to know if you can cook. I need to know if you can work.”**
- Required Culinary 2 to recalibrate every cohort to the current WACOS operating system regardless of prior course location/date; prior experience no longer substitutes for current-system training, though the instructor may accelerate demonstrated competence.
- Made Week 4 outcome-based rather than a rigid day-by-day sequence. Required pre-opening experiences now include whole-group Service simulation, Barista operating-system practice, Practice Bistro Prep, Production Preflight, clean-stop/handoff practice, whole-operation Reset & Closing rehearsal, cumulative safety application, and at least one complete mock-service cycle that may be brief/simplified.
- Made Thursday Prep normally individual production work, with pairs when justified, while retaining Monday-Thursday cleaning jobs. Added a brief Bistro Huddle and **No checkoff → no production** Production Preflight.
- Clarified Clean as You Go vs Reset vs Close and made situational awareness a five-team expectation using **What do you notice? What does it mean? What needs to happen next?**
- Made Bistro Prep and Service cumulative food-safety/workplace-safety performance environments; Culinary 2 moves toward **safety fluency under pressure**.

### Week 5 Implementation Evidence

- First public service produced approximately $1,000 in sales across three classes; food quality and demand were strong, multiple products sold out, and operating capacity rather than customer interest limited additional sales.
- Thursday Prep exposed missing independent production-management skills: students began before reading, changed scale without authorization, confused units/quantities, skipped mise/preflight, and required substantial adult rescue.
- Friday exposed weak Service circulation/guest care, Barista instability, queue-number breakdowns, safety/sanitation concerns, incomplete reset/close, and order-control problems despite successful food production.
- Initial FOH/BOH training had been split between instructor and Teaching Assistant; future initial Service system training is whole-group and instructor-led, with the Teaching Assistant reinforcing/coaching after the system is established.
- The immediate Week 6 recovery delays knife-skills opening for targeted systems correction: Service Monday; Reset/Closing + Barista Tuesday; Practice Bistro Prep Wednesday; real Prep Thursday; Bistro Service Friday.

### Operational Follow-Up

- Opened separate follow-up for an approved OnTime/Kiosk failure fallback and for reliable prompt Square entry of accepted phone/email/remote orders. No district attendance or financial controls were invented or changed.
