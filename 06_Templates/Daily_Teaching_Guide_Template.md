# Weekly Teaching Guide — Prototype E

> Adopted instructor/Teaching Assistant working format. Use one weekly overview page, then one separate full page for each instructional day. A normal Monday-Friday guide is six pages. Use week/day identifiers without calendar dates. Follow the learning sequence, adjust to the available block, and record where each section stops. Student-facing materials require instructor review before use.

## Print Setup

US Letter portrait; 0.5-inch margins; Arial; black text; blue headings (`#366091`). Use 20 pt bold centered titles, 13 pt bold section headings, 12 pt body/subtitle, and 11 pt supporting notes. Single line spacing; balance paragraph/section spacing to fill the printable area. Increase spacing or type on shorter pages as useful. Use an explicit page break before each daily title. Keep headings with their content; retain usable note-writing space. Render and inspect every page.

Dense pages require concise wording and references to governing recipes/SOPs, while preserving essential learning, safety, and authorization. Do not shrink body text to force a fit.

See the [standard](../09_Workflows/Daily_Teaching_Guide_Standard.md) and [design guidance](../09_Workflows/Document_Design_Standard.md).

### Students Do

Essential learning and authentic individual performance evidence. Include the opening task, immediate plan, and required safety/authorization gates where they belong.

### Micro-Skills / If They Come Up

Expected just-in-time micro-skills and useful refinements. Keep these compact rather than making every micro-skill a separate objective, worksheet, or grade.

Likely errors, safety concerns, and quality/understanding checks.

### Clean Stop
