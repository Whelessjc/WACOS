# Published Artifact Index

## Purpose

This is the lightweight WACOS record of instructor-reviewed artifacts published for direct school use. It records enough information to locate and govern an artifact without duplicating the Drive library or tracking routine file activity.

## Status Values

- `Draft` - creation has started but instructor review is incomplete.
- `In Review` - ready for instructor review.
- `Published` - accepted and available at the listed Drive destination.
- `Revise` - previously usable but needs review or correction before further use.
- `Retired` - retained only when historical context or replacement tracking is useful.

## Index

| Artifact name | Course or operation | Week/unit | Governing WACOS source | Delivery format | Drive destination or stable link | Status | Last reviewed | Notes or next review |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Add entries as artifacts are reviewed and published. Do not infer that existing repository export folders or unverified external files are published merely because they exist.

## Maintenance

- Prefer one row for a reviewed packet or bundle when that adequately represents its components.
- Use a Drive folder path when a stable link is unavailable; update broken or moved links during normal review.
- Formatting-only Drive revisions do not require an index update unless the review date, status, format, or destination changes.
- Route durable curriculum or operating changes through the [WACOS Update Workflow](../00_Core/WACOS_Update_Workflow.md).
